Name: Chile, mild red: Aleppo
Aka: Aleppo pepper, Aleppo chile, Halaby pepper, Aleppo flakes, Maras pepper
Category: Chiles
Family: Chile, mild red
Form: flakes
Origin: Syria, Turkey
Cuisines: Syrian, Turkish, Levantine
Flavor: fruity, cumin-like, mildly salty, tangy
Power: 2
Heat: 2
Tip: Use it as a finishing flake or in marinades. It gives gentle heat and a fruity tang, and it is often salted and oily.
Pairs with: Lamb, Eggs, Tomatoes, Dips and spreads
Typical dishes: muhammara, kebabs, shakshuka
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleppo_pepper
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Maras pepper is a Turkish cousin that I grouped here as an alias. The Heat rating is from a published range of about 10,000 Scoville units that I recalled and did not confirm.
