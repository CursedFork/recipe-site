Name: Allspice
Aka: pimento, Jamaica pepper, ground allspice, allspice berries
Category: Warm spices
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Jamaica, Guatemala
Cuisines: Jamaican, Caribbean, Levantine
Flavor: warm, peppery, clove-like, sweet
Power: 3
Tip: One berry or a pinch tastes like cinnamon, nutmeg and clove together, which is why it is named allspice.
Pairs with: Pork, Beef, Sausage, Cakes and muffins, Mulled wine and cider
Typical dishes: jerk seasoning, Cincinnati chili, pumpkin pie
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allspice
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Its strength sits between Power 3 and Power 4, and I chose 3. The origin note covers where it grows.
