Name: Thyme
Aka: dried thyme, thyme leaves, common thyme
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves
Origin: Greece, Spain
Cuisines: French, Italian, Levantine
Flavor: earthy, minty, lemony, slightly floral
Power: 2
Tip: Add early to soups, stews and roasts, because the leaves are tough and the flavor takes time to release.
Pairs with: Chicken, Potatoes, Mushrooms, Stews and braises
Typical dishes: roast chicken, bouquet garni, herbes de Provence
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thyme
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The origin is the general Mediterranean basin, and I picked Greece and Spain as the countries the page names.
