Name: Dried lime
Aka: loomi, limoo amani, black lime, noomi basra, dried limes
Category: Tangy and floral
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Iran, Oman
Cuisines: Persian, Iraqi, Gulf Arab
Flavor: sour, earthy, fermented, slightly bitter
Power: 4
Tip: Pierce a whole dried lime and simmer it in stews and soups, or grind it for a sour powder. Too much turns bitter.
Pairs with: Stews and braises, Lamb, Rice, Lentils
Typical dishes: khoresh, Gulf kabsa, Iraqi stews
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dried_lime
Confidence: low
Doubt: The page places its origin in the Persian Gulf, and the Power rating is mine, from comparison with other sours.
