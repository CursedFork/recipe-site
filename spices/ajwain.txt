Name: Ajwain
Aka: carom seed, carom seeds, ajowan, bishop's weed, ajwain seeds
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole
Origin: India
Cuisines: Indian, Pakistani, Ethiopian
Flavor: thyme-like, sharp, bitter, pungent
Power: 4
Tip: Crush the seeds between your fingers and add them to hot oil or dough. They taste like strong thyme, so a pinch is plenty.
Pairs with: Flatbread, Lentils, Potatoes, Cauliflower and broccoli
Typical dishes: paratha, pakoras, namak para
