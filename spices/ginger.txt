Name: Ginger
Aka: ground ginger, dried ginger, ginger powder
Category: Roots and rhizomes
Form: ground
Origin: India, China
Cuisines: Chinese, Indian, Jamaican, British
Flavor: warm, peppery, citrusy
Power: 3
Tip: Dried ground ginger is warmer and less bright than fresh. Use it in baking, spice blends and marinades, and about a quarter as much as fresh.
Pairs with: Pork, Carrots, Cookies, Sweet breads and buns, Tea and chai
Typical dishes: gingerbread, curries, pumpkin pie spice
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ginger
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The Power rating is mine. The quarter-of-fresh conversion is a common rule of thumb that I could not source.
