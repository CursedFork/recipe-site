Name: Black cardamom
Aka: brown cardamom, black cardamom pods, badi elaichi
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole
Origin: Nepal, India
Cuisines: Indian, Nepali, Chinese
Flavor: smoky, camphor-like, resinous, earthy
Power: 4
Tip: Drop a whole pod into rice, braises or stock and lift it out before serving. It is smoky, not floral like green cardamom.
Pairs with: Pilaf and biryani, Lamb, Lentils, Beef
Typical dishes: biryani, garam masala, Sichuan hot pot
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amomum_subulatum
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The origin countries are broad pointers from general knowledge. The Power rating is mine, and the pods vary a lot in strength.
