Name: Garlic powder
Aka: granulated garlic, garlic granules, dried garlic
Category: Alliums and aromatics
Form: ground, granules
Origin: Uzbekistan, India
Cuisines: Global
Flavor: savory, pungent, toasty
Power: 3
Tip: Mix into rubs and breading, where fresh garlic would burn. Use about a quarter of the amount of fresh cloves.
Pairs with: Chicken, Beef, Potatoes, Chips and crackers
Typical dishes: dry rubs, garlic bread, seasoned fries
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garlic_powder
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says garlic probably originated in Central Asia or South Asia, so the two origins are broad pointers. Garlic powder itself is made wherever garlic is grown, mostly China today. The one-quarter-of-fresh rule is a common kitchen rule of thumb that I could not source.
