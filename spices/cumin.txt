Name: Cumin
Aka: cumin seed, ground cumin, jeera
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Iran, India
Cuisines: Indian, Mexican, Levantine, Moroccan
Flavor: earthy, warm, nutty, slightly bitter
Power: 3
Tip: Toast whole seeds in a dry pan until fragrant, or bloom ground cumin in oil. It is a base note, so use it early.
Pairs with: Beef, Lamb, Chickpeas, Black beans, Rice
Typical dishes: chili, tacos, hummus, curries
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumin
Confidence: high
