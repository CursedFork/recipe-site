Name: Long pepper
Aka: pippali, Indian long pepper, long peppercorns, Javanese long pepper
Category: Peppercorns
Form: whole, ground
Origin: India, Indonesia
Cuisines: Indian, Nepali
Flavor: hot, sweet, peppery, cinnamon-like
Power: 4
Tip: Crush or grate the catkins. It is hotter and sweeter than black pepper and was widely used before black pepper took over.
Pairs with: Lamb, Lentils
Typical dishes: Ayurvedic spice mixes, pickles, Nepali dishes
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piper_longum
Confidence: low
Doubt: A rare spice that I rated by comparison with black pepper. The page names Java and India.
