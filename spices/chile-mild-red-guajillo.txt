Name: Chile, mild red: Guajillo
Aka: guajillo chile, guajillo chili, chile guajillo, guajillo powder
Category: Chiles
Family: Chile, mild red
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Mexico
Cuisines: Mexican, Tex-Mex
Flavor: tangy, fruity, berry-like, slightly smoky
Power: 2

Pairs with: Pork, Beef, Chicken, Tomato sauces
Typical dishes: pozole rojo, birria, tamales
