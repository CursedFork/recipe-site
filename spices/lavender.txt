Name: Lavender
Aka: dried lavender, culinary lavender, lavender buds
Category: Tangy and floral
Form: whole
Origin: France, Spain
Cuisines: French, British
Flavor: floral, perfumed, herbal, slightly bitter
Power: 4
Tip: Use food-grade buds and very little, because it turns soapy fast. Grind it into sugar or steep it in cream and strain.
Pairs with: Cookies, Cakes and muffins, Ice cream and frozen desserts, Lamb
Typical dishes: herbes de Provence, shortbread, ice cream
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavandula
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says lavender is native to the Iberian Peninsula and the Mediterranean coast. The Power rating is mine.
