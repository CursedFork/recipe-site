Name: Black salt
Aka: kala namak, Himalayan black salt, sulfur salt
Category: Salts and sugars
Form: granules, ground
Origin: India, Pakistan
Cuisines: Indian, Pakistani
Flavor: salty, sulfurous, eggy, tangy
Tip: Sprinkle on at the end, as a finishing salt. The sulfur smell is what makes it taste like egg in vegan dishes.
Pairs with: Chickpeas, Tofu, Tomatoes, Dips and spreads
Typical dishes: chaat, raita, tofu scramble
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kala_namak
Confidence: high
