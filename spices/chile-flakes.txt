Name: Chile flakes
Aka: red pepper flakes, crushed red pepper, chili flakes, chilli flakes, crushed chile
Category: Chiles
Form: flakes
Origin: Italy, Turkey
Cuisines: Italian, Turkish, American
Flavor: hot, fruity, slightly smoky
Power: 3
Heat: 3
Tip: Sprinkle on at the table or bloom in warm oil at the start so the heat spreads through the dish.
Pairs with: Pizza, Pasta, Scrambled and fried eggs, Tomato sauces
Typical dishes: pizza, aglio e olio, arrabbiata
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crushed_red_pepper
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Flakes are a blend of chile types, so heat varies by brand. I set Heat to 3 for the usual cayenne-type pepper flakes. Aleppo or Maras flakes are milder.
