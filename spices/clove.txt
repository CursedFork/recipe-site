Name: Clove
Aka: cloves, whole cloves, ground cloves, ground clove
Category: Warm spices
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Indonesia
Cuisines: Indonesian, Indian, Dutch
Flavor: warm, sweet, sharply aromatic, almost medicinal
Power: 5
Tip: A very little goes a long way, so start with one or two whole cloves in a pot or a pinch of ground. Fish whole cloves out before serving.
Pairs with: Ham, Curry, Apples and pears
Typical dishes: baked ham, curries, pumpkin pie spice, speculaas, hot spiced drinks

Confidence: high
