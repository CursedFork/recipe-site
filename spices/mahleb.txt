Name: Mahleb
Aka: mahlab, mahlepi, St. Lucie cherry, mahleb seeds
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Turkey, Syria
Cuisines: Levantine, Turkish, Greek
Flavor: cherry and almond-like, floral, bitter-sweet
Power: 3
Tip: Grind the small kernels fresh, since the aroma fades quickly. A pinch flavors a whole batch of bread or cookies.
Pairs with: Sweet breads and buns, Cookies, Cakes and muffins, Bread and toast
Typical dishes: tsoureki, ka'ak, maamoul
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prunus_mahaleb
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says the tree is native to a wide range from Europe to central Asia, so the two origin countries are broad pointers. The Power rating is mine.
