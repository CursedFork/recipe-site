Name: Star anise
Aka: star aniseed, whole star anise, ground star anise
Category: Warm spices
Form: whole, ground
Origin: China, Vietnam
Cuisines: Chinese, Vietnamese, Indian
Flavor: sweet, licorice, warm, aromatic
Power: 4
Tip: One or two stars flavor a whole pot of broth or braise. Lift them out before serving.
Pairs with: Pork, Beef, Broth and stock, Noodles
Typical dishes: pho, red-cooked pork, five-spice powder, mulled wine
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illicium_verum
Confidence: high
