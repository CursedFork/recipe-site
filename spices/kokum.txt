Name: Kokum
Aka: kokam, kokum rind, dried kokum, Garcinia indica
Category: Tangy and floral
Form: whole
Origin: India
Cuisines: Indian
Flavor: sour, fruity, slightly sweet, mild
Power: 2
Tip: Soak or simmer a few dried rinds in curries, dals and drinks for a mild sourness. It is a gentler stand-in for tamarind.
Pairs with: Fish, Lentils, Shrimp, Curry
Typical dishes: Goan fish curry, solkadhi, amti
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garcinia_indica
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The Power rating is mine, and I rated it low because the sour is soft. The page says the tree is native to the forests of India.
