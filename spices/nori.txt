Name: Nori
Aka: seaweed, laver, nori sheets, toasted seaweed, nori flakes
Category: Other ingredients
Form: flakes, leaves
Origin: Japan
Cuisines: Japanese, Korean
Flavor: savory, oceanic, umami, toasty
Tip: Crumble or snip toasted nori over rice, eggs and noodles at the end. It is salty, so taste before adding more salt.
Pairs with: Rice, Noodles, Scrambled and fried eggs, Popcorn
Typical dishes: furikake, sushi, onigiri
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nori
Confidence: high
