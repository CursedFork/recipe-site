Name: Chile, mild red: Urfa
Aka: Urfa biber, Urfa pepper, Urfa chile, isot pepper, isot
Category: Chiles
Family: Chile, mild red
Form: flakes
Origin: Turkey
Cuisines: Turkish
Flavor: smoky, raisin-like, earthy, chocolatey
Power: 2
Heat: 1
Tip: A dark, oily flake with a smoky-sweet taste. Sprinkle it on at the table, or add to kebabs and eggs.
Pairs with: Lamb, Eggs, Tomatoes, Dips and spreads
Typical dishes: Turkish kebabs, hummus, roasted vegetables
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urfa_biber
Confidence: medium
Doubt: I set Heat to 1 from sellers who describe it as mild. Published figures vary, and some place it higher.
