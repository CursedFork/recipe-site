Name: Mustard seed
Aka: yellow mustard seed, brown mustard seed, black mustard seed, mustard seeds
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole, ground
Origin: India, Canada
Cuisines: Indian, American, German
Flavor: pungent, nutty, sharp when crushed
Power: 3
Allergen: Mustard
Tip: Pop whole seeds in hot oil to mellow them into a nutty crackle. Crush or grind them with liquid for sharpness.
Pairs with: Pork, Potatoes, Cabbage and Brussels sprouts, Salad dressings
Typical dishes: tadka for dal, pickles, mustard
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mustard_seed
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Canada is today's largest grower, not a culinary origin. Power is mine, and brown and black seeds are hotter than yellow.
