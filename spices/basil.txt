Name: Basil
Aka: dried basil, sweet basil, basil leaves
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves
Origin: India
Cuisines: Italian, Thai, Greek
Flavor: sweet, peppery, clove-like, minty
Power: 2
Tip: Dried basil is milder and more hay-like than fresh. Add it early to sauces, and keep fresh basil for the end.
Pairs with: Tomatoes, Pasta, Pizza, Scrambled and fried eggs
Typical dishes: tomato sauce, pizza sauce, pesto
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basil
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Basil dries poorly and varies a lot by brand, so Power 2 is a middle guess. Thai basil is a different plant with an anise flavor.
