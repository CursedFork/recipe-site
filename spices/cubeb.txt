Name: Cubeb
Aka: cubeb pepper, tailed pepper, Java pepper
Category: Peppercorns
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Indonesia
Cuisines: Indonesian, Moroccan
Flavor: peppery, allspice-like, pine, slightly bitter
Power: 4
Tip: Crush the berries just before use. It tastes like pepper with a hint of pine and allspice.
Pairs with: Lamb, Chicken, Rice, Stews and braises
Typical dishes: ras el hanout, Moroccan tagines
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubeb
Confidence: low
Doubt: A rare spice I rated by comparison and not from a published figure. The page confirms Java and Sumatra.
