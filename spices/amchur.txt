Name: Amchur
Aka: amchoor, dried mango powder, mango powder, aamchur
Category: Tangy and floral
Form: ground
Origin: India
Cuisines: Indian, Pakistani
Flavor: sour, fruity, tangy, slightly sweet
Power: 2
Tip: Use it where you want sourness without liquid, such as dry vegetable dishes, marinades and chaat. It is made from unripe mango.
Pairs with: Chickpeas, Potatoes, Cauliflower and broccoli, Lentils
Typical dishes: chaat masala, aloo gobi, samosa filling
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amchur
Confidence: high
