Name: Caraway
Aka: caraway seed, caraway seeds, ground caraway
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Germany, Turkey
Cuisines: German, Eastern European, Hungarian, Moroccan
Flavor: earthy, anise-like, rye-bread, slightly peppery
Power: 3
Tip: Toast and crush lightly. It is the taste of rye bread, and it cuts through rich meat and cabbage.
Pairs with: Pork, Cabbage and Brussels sprouts, Bread and toast, Cheese
Typical dishes: rye bread, sauerkraut, goulash, harissa
