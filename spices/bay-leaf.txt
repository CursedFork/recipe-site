Name: Bay leaf
Aka: bay leaves, bay laurel, laurel leaf, dried bay
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves
Origin: Turkey, Greece
Cuisines: French, Italian, Indian, Caribbean
Flavor: herbal, tea-like, faintly floral
Power: 2
Tip: Drop one or two whole leaves into soups, braises and stocks, and take them out before serving. They are not meant to be eaten.
Pairs with: Soup, Stews and braises, Beans and lentils, Broth and stock
Typical dishes: bouquet garni, bean soup, braises
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_leaf
Confidence: high
