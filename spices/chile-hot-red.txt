Name: Chile, hot red
Aka: hot red chile, hot red chili, hot chile powder
Category: Chiles
Form: whole, ground, flakes
Origin: Mexico
Cuisines: Mexican, Indian, Thai, Sichuan
Flavor: hot, sharp, bright
Power: 4
Heat: 3
Tip: This is the general entry for a hot dried red chile when you do not care which. Start with a pinch, taste, and add more.
Pairs with: Chicken, Beef, Shrimp, Chili, Curry
Typical dishes: hot sauces, curries, chili
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chili_pepper
Confidence: medium
Doubt: This umbrella stands for any hot dried red chile, so its Power and Heat are a middle guess. Your own cayenne, arbol or similar may be hotter or milder.
