Name: Chile, hot red: Arbol
Aka: chile de arbol, arbol chile, arbol chili, tree chile
Category: Chiles
Family: Chile, hot red
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Mexico
Cuisines: Mexican, Tex-Mex
Flavor: hot, sharp, nutty, grassy
Power: 4
Heat: 3
Tip: Toast the whole dried pods briefly and blend them into salsas, or use them whole in oil. Hotter than guajillo.
Pairs with: Beef, Tomatoes, Pork, Barbecue and hot sauces
Typical dishes: salsa roja, hot sauces, pozole
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chile_de_%C3%A1rbol
Confidence: medium
