Name: Mace
Aka: ground mace, blade mace
Category: Warm spices
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Indonesia
Cuisines: Dutch, Indian, British
Flavor: warm, delicate, nutmeg-like, citrusy
Power: 4
Tip: Use as you would nutmeg, but expect a lighter, more citrusy note. A blade simmers in stock and is lifted out.
Pairs with: Milk and cream, Sausage, Cakes and muffins
Typical dishes: donuts, pound cake, pickling spice, potted shrimp
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mace_(spice)
Confidence: low
Doubt: Mace comes from the same fruit as nutmeg and is a little gentler. Power 4 against 3 is my guess and could be wrong in either direction.
