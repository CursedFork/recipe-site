Name: Curry leaf
Aka: curry leaves, dried curry leaves, kadi patta
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves
Origin: India, Sri Lanka
Cuisines: Indian, Sri Lankan
Flavor: citrusy, nutty, slightly bitter, savory
Power: 2
Tip: Fry whole leaves in hot oil at the start. Fresh leaves are far better than dried, which have a faint flavor.
Pairs with: Lentils, Rice
Typical dishes: sambar, dal tadka, South Indian chutneys
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curry_tree
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Dried curry leaves are much weaker than fresh, so the rating is for dried. The page says the tree is native to the Indian subcontinent and parts of Asia.
