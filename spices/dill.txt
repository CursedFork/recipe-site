Name: Dill
Aka: dill weed, dried dill, dill seed, dill seeds
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves, whole
Origin: Iran, Egypt
Cuisines: Scandinavian, Russian, Greek, Polish
Flavor: fresh, grassy, anise-like, tangy
Power: 2
Tip: Dill weed is the feathery leaf and is delicate, so add it near the end. The seed is a separate, stronger flavor.
Pairs with: Salmon, Potatoes, Yogurt, Cabbage and Brussels sprouts
Typical dishes: pickles, gravlax, tzatziki, borscht
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dill
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says dill is native to North Africa, West Asia and Iran, so the two origins are broad pointers. Seed and weed are different tastes and strengths. I covered both in one entry at Power 2, which fits the weed better than the seed.
