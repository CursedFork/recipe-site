Name: Pepper, white
Aka: white pepper, ground white pepper, white peppercorns
Category: Peppercorns
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Vietnam, Indonesia
Cuisines: Chinese, French, Scandinavian
Flavor: earthy, sharp, musty, hot
Power: 4
Tip: White pepper is black pepper with the dark skin removed, so it disappears into pale sauces and soups. It tastes sharper and more earthy.
Pairs with: Cream soups and chowders, Mayonnaise and creamy sauces, Potatoes, White fish
Typical dishes: hot and sour soup, bechamel, mashed potatoes
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_pepper
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The origin countries are where most white pepper is made today, which I took from general knowledge and not from a page I read. The Power rating is mine.
