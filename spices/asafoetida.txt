Name: Asafoetida
Aka: hing, asafetida, hingu, devil's dung
Category: Alliums and aromatics
Form: ground, granules
Origin: Iran, Afghanistan
Cuisines: Indian
Flavor: pungent, sulfurous raw, savory like onion and garlic when cooked
Power: 5
Tip: Fry a tiny pinch in hot oil for a few seconds at the start of cooking. Raw, the smell is strong, but it turns savory in the oil.
Pairs with: Lentils, Cauliflower and broccoli, Chickpeas, Potatoes
Typical dishes: dal tadka, sambar, pakoras
Careful: Many brands are cut with wheat flour, so check the label if you avoid gluten.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asafoetida
Confidence: high
