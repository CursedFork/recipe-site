Name: Chervil
Aka: dried chervil, French parsley
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves
Origin: Russia
Cuisines: French
Flavor: delicate, mild anise, parsley-like
Power: 1
Tip: Very delicate and loses flavor when dried. Add it at the end, not the start.
Pairs with: Scrambled and fried eggs, Salmon, Chicken, Mayonnaise and creamy sauces
Typical dishes: fines herbes, bearnaise, omelets
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chervil
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Dried chervil is weak, so Power 1 is generous. The origin is a broad pointer from general knowledge.
