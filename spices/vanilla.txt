Name: Vanilla
Aka: vanilla bean, vanilla pod, vanilla extract, vanilla powder
Category: Other ingredients
Form: whole
Origin: Mexico, Madagascar
Cuisines: French, Mexican, American
Flavor: sweet, creamy, floral, warm
Tip: Split a bean and scrape out the seeds, or use extract. Add it at the end of heating so the flavor is not boiled away.
Pairs with: Custards and puddings, Cakes and muffins, Ice cream and frozen desserts, Berries
Typical dishes: custard, ice cream, cakes
