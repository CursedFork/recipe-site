Name: Fenugreek leaves
Aka: kasuri methi, dried fenugreek leaves, methi leaves, dried methi
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves
Origin: India
Cuisines: Indian, Pakistani
Flavor: bitter, maple-like, savory, celery-like
Power: 3
Tip: Rub the dried leaves between your palms to crush them and add them at the end of cooking. They are not the same as the seeds.
Pairs with: Chicken, Curry, Potatoes, Flatbread
Typical dishes: butter chicken, paneer dishes, methi paratha
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenugreek
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The Power rating is mine. The page covers the whole plant, and kasuri methi is a regional product from Kasur in Pakistan.
