Name: Savory
Aka: summer savory, winter savory, dried savory
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves
Origin: Italy
Cuisines: French, German, Eastern European
Flavor: peppery, thyme-like, minty, piney
Power: 3
Tip: Known as the bean herb. Add to beans, lentils and sausages early, since the leaves are tough.
Pairs with: Kidney and pinto beans, White beans, Sausage, Green beans and peas
Typical dishes: herbes de Provence, bean soups, sausage
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satureja_hortensis
Confidence: medium
