Name: Nigella
Aka: kalonji, black cumin, black seed, nigella seeds, charnushka
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole
Origin: Turkey, Iran
Cuisines: Indian, Bangladeshi, Turkish, Levantine
Flavor: peppery, onion-like, bitter, slightly smoky
Power: 3
Tip: Sprinkle on flatbread before baking, or pop in hot oil. It is not the same plant as cumin, even though one name is black cumin.
Pairs with: Flatbread, Bread and toast, Lentils, Cauliflower and broccoli
Typical dishes: naan, panch phoron, Turkish pastries
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigella_sativa
Confidence: high
