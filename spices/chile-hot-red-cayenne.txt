Name: Chile, hot red: Cayenne
Aka: cayenne, cayenne pepper, ground cayenne
Category: Chiles

Origin: Mexico
Cuisines: Cajun and Creole, Mexican, Indian
Flavor: hot, sharp, clean, lightly fruity
