Name: Cardamom
Aka: green cardamom, cardamom pods, cardamom seeds, ground cardamom
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole, ground
Origin: India, Guatemala
Cuisines: Indian, Scandinavian, Gulf Arab
Flavor: floral, citrusy, minty, sweet-resinous
Power: 4
Tip: Crack a pod and use the seeds, or grind them fresh. A few seeds flavor a whole pot of rice, a custard or a batch of buns.
Pairs with: Rice, Pilaf and biryani, Lamb, Cakes and muffins, Tea and chai, Coffee and hot chocolate
Typical dishes: chai, biryani, Swedish buns, Arabic coffee
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardamom
Confidence: high
