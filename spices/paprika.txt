Name: Paprika
Aka: sweet paprika, ground paprika, Hungarian paprika
Category: Paprika
Form: ground
Origin: Hungary, Spain
Cuisines: Hungarian, Spanish, American
Flavor: sweet, mild, fruity, earthy
Power: 1
Heat: 0
Tip: Paprika scorches fast. Stir it into oil off the heat, or add it with liquid. It gives color and sweetness more than heat.
Pairs with: Chicken, Potatoes, Boiled and deviled eggs, Stews and braises
Typical dishes: goulash, deviled eggs, roast chicken
