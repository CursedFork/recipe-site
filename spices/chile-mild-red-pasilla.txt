Name: Chile, mild red: Pasilla
Aka: pasilla chile, chile negro, pasilla negro, pasilla powder
Category: Chiles
Family: Chile, mild red
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Mexico
Cuisines: Mexican
Flavor: mild, raisin-like, earthy, cocoa-like
Power: 2
Heat: 1
Tip: Toast the pods lightly and soak, then blend them into sauces. Ground pasilla works in rubs and moles.
Pairs with: Pork, Chicken, Beef, Tomato sauces
Typical dishes: mole negro, adobo sauces, enchilada sauce
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasilla
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The Heat rating is from a published range of about 1,000 to 2,500 Scoville units that I recalled and did not confirm.
