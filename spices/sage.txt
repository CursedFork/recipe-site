Name: Sage
Aka: dried sage, rubbed sage, ground sage, common sage
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves, ground
Origin: Italy, Greece
Cuisines: Italian, American, British
Flavor: earthy, musky, piney, slightly bitter
Power: 4
Tip: Strong and slightly drying, so use less than you think. It likes browned butter and fat.
Pairs with: Pork, Sausage, Turkey, Butter and ghee
Typical dishes: stuffing, sausage, brown butter sauce
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvia_officinalis
Confidence: medium
Doubt: I rated Power 4 because rubbed sage is strong in small amounts, but it is a judgment call. The Mediterranean origin is broad.
