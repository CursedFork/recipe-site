Name: Saffron
Aka: saffron threads, saffron strands, crocus saffron
Category: Tangy and floral
Form: whole
Origin: Iran, Spain
Cuisines: Persian, Spanish, Indian, Italian
Flavor: floral, honey-like, hay-like, slightly bitter
Power: 5
Tip: Steep a pinch of threads in warm water or milk for fifteen minutes first, then add the liquid. Too much tastes medicinal.
Pairs with: Rice, Pilaf and biryani, Crab and lobster, Custards and puddings
Typical dishes: paella, biryani, bouillabaisse, risotto Milanese
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saffron
Confidence: low
Doubt: Power 4 or 5 is a judgment call. I chose 5 because a few threads are enough for four servings, but its cost makes cooks use it carefully either way.
