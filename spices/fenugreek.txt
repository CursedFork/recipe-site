Name: Fenugreek
Aka: fenugreek seed, fenugreek seeds, methi, ground fenugreek
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole, ground
Origin: India, Egypt
Cuisines: Indian, Ethiopian, Persian
Flavor: bittersweet, maple-like, nutty, celery-like
Power: 4
Tip: Use sparingly and toast lightly, because too much turns a dish bitter. Dried leaves, kasuri methi, are a separate, milder ingredient.
Pairs with: Curry, Lentils, Cauliflower and broccoli, Chicken
Typical dishes: curry powder, dal, berbere
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenugreek
Confidence: high
