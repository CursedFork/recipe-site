Name: Coriander
Aka: coriander seed, ground coriander, coriander seeds
Category: Seeds and pods
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Greece
Cuisines: Indian, Levantine, Mexican
Flavor: citrusy, mild, sweet-woody
Power: 2
Tip: Toast the seeds briefly before grinding. This is the seed of the cilantro plant, and it tastes lemony and warm, not like the fresh leaf.
Pairs with: Pork, Lamb, Lentils, Carrots
Typical dishes: curry powder, falafel, garam masala
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coriander
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says coriander is native to the Mediterranean Basin, which covers many countries, so Greece (where the oldest written name appears) is a broad pointer.
