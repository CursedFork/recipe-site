Name: Nutmeg
Aka: ground nutmeg, whole nutmeg
Category: Warm spices
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Indonesia, Grenada
Cuisines: Dutch, Indian, Caribbean, Italian
Flavor: warm, sweet, nutty, woody
Power: 4
Tip: Grate whole nutmeg fresh with a fine grater. A light grating finishes cream sauces, custards and greens.
Pairs with: Milk and cream, Leafy greens, Potatoes, Custards and puddings, Mulled wine and cider
Typical dishes: bechamel, eggnog, pumpkin pie, ravioli filling
Careful: Large amounts are toxic, so keep to a pinch or a light grating.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutmeg
Confidence: high
