Name: Grains of paradise
Aka: melegueta pepper, alligator pepper, Guinea pepper, Aframomum melegueta
Category: Peppercorns
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Ghana, Nigeria
Cuisines: West African, Moroccan, Scandinavian
Flavor: peppery, citrusy, floral, gingery
Power: 3
Tip: Grind it like pepper. It has black pepper's heat with a floral, citrus edge.
Pairs with: Beef, Lamb, Salad dressings, Stews and braises
Typical dishes: ras el hanout, Scandinavian aquavit, West African stews
