Name: Salt
Aka: table salt, kosher salt, sea salt, salt flakes
Category: Salts and sugars
Form: whole, ground, flakes
Origin: Romania
Cuisines: Global
Flavor: salty, mineral
Tip: Season in small steps and taste. Kosher and flaky salts are less dense than table salt, so a spoonful of table salt is much saltier.
Pairs with: Scrambled and fried eggs, Potatoes, Steak, Pasta
Typical dishes: nearly everything
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salt
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Salt has no single origin. I listed the place the page names for the earliest known salt production.
