Name: Cinnamon
Aka: cassia, Ceylon cinnamon, cinnamon sticks, ground cinnamon, canela
Category: Warm spices
Form: whole, ground
Origin: Sri Lanka, China, Indonesia
Cuisines: Global
Flavor: warm, sweet, woody
Power: 3
Tip: Ground cinnamon goes into batters, oatmeal and rubs. A whole stick simmers in drinks, rice and braises and is lifted out at the end.
Pairs with: Apples and pears, Oats and porridge, Lamb, Cakes and muffins, Tea and chai
Typical dishes: cinnamon rolls, apple pie, tagines, garam masala
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinnamon
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Ceylon cinnamon is milder and cassia is stronger and more pungent. I rated them together at 3. Ceylon alone might be 2, and strong cassia could be 4.
