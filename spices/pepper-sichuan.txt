Name: Pepper, Sichuan
Aka: Sichuan pepper, Szechuan pepper, Sichuan peppercorns, Szechuan peppercorns
Category: Peppercorns
Form: whole, ground
Origin: China
Cuisines: Sichuan, Chinese, Nepali
Flavor: citrusy, floral, numbing, tingly
Power: 4
Tip: Toast the husks, then grind and sift out the black seeds. The numbing tingle is not heat, so it pairs well with chiles.
Pairs with: Pork, Tofu, Chicken, Noodles
Typical dishes: mapo tofu, dan dan noodles, five-spice powder
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan_pepper
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The Power rating is mine. Its tingle is a different sensation than heat, so it carries no Heat rating.
