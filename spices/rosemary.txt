Name: Rosemary
Aka: dried rosemary, rosemary leaves, rosemary needles
Category: Dried herbs
Form: leaves
Origin: Italy, Spain
Cuisines: Italian, French, Greek
Flavor: piney, resinous, woody, lemony
Power: 3
Tip: Chop the needles finely or crush them, because whole dried needles are sharp and tough. It stands up to long roasting.
Pairs with: Lamb, Potatoes, Chicken, Bread and toast
Typical dishes: roast lamb, focaccia, roast potatoes
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosemary
Confidence: high
