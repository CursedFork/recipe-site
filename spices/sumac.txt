Name: Sumac
Aka: ground sumac, sumac berries
Category: Tangy and floral
Form: ground
Origin: Turkey, Iran, Lebanon
Cuisines: Levantine, Turkish, Persian
Flavor: tart, lemony, fruity, a little astringent
Power: 2
Tip: Sprinkle it on at the end for a bright lemony tang without adding liquid. It is salty in some brands, so taste first.
Pairs with: Chicken, Lamb, Salad dressings, Onions and leeks
Typical dishes: zaatar, fattoush, kebabs
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sumac
Confidence: high
