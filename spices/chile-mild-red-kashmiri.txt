Name: Chile, mild red: Kashmiri
Aka: Kashmiri chili, Kashmiri chile, Kashmiri chilli powder, Kashmiri mirch
Category: Chiles
Family: Chile, mild red
Form: whole, ground
Origin: India
Cuisines: Indian
Flavor: mild, fruity, sweet, deep red
Power: 2
Heat: 1
Tip: Used mostly for its brilliant red color. It brightens tandoori marinades and curries with very little heat.
Pairs with: Chicken, Lamb, Curry, Lentils
Typical dishes: tandoori chicken, rogan josh, butter chicken
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashmiri_chili
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The Heat rating comes from a published range for this chile of roughly 1,000 to 2,000 Scoville units, which I recalled and did not confirm on a page.
