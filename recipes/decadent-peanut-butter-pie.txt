Title: Decadent Peanut Butter Pie
Cuisine: American
Regions: United States
Meal: dessert
Healthy: no
Prep: 15
Cook: 1
Servings: 8

Ingredients:
For the pie:
1 chocolate cookie pie crust, prepared
1 cup creamy peanut butter
1/2 cup sugar
8 oz cream cheese, at room temperature
4 1/2 cups non-dairy whipped topping, thawed and divided
11.75 oz hot fudge topping
For the drizzle:
hot fudge topping, 2 tbsp reserved from the jar above
2 tbsp creamy peanut butter

Instructions:
1. In a medium bowl, mix the peanut butter, cream cheese, and sugar until smooth.
2. Gently fold in 3 cups of the whipped topping.
3. Spoon the mixture into the pie crust and use a spatula to spread it out to the edges.
4. Set aside 2 tablespoons of the hot fudge topping for the drizzle. Pour the rest into a microwave-safe bowl or glass measuring cup, microwave it for 1 minute, and stir.
5. Spread the warm hot fudge over the peanut butter layer so it covers the whole surface.
6. Refrigerate the pie for at least 2 hours, or until you are ready to serve.
7. Just before serving, spread the remaining 1 1/2 cups of whipped topping over the fudge layer, taking care not to mix the two layers.
8. Put the reserved 2 tablespoons of hot fudge in a small plastic bag and knead it for a few seconds. Snip a tiny hole in one corner and drizzle the fudge over the pie.
9. Repeat with the 2 tablespoons of peanut butter, drizzling it in the opposite direction from the fudge.

Notes:
Allergens: peanuts, milk, wheat (gluten), soybeans. This is based only on the ingredients as written, so check your product labels, since chocolate cookie crusts often contain wheat and soy, non-dairy whipped toppings can still contain milk-derived ingredients, hot fudge toppings often contain soy, and cream cheese contains milk, and think about cross-contact.
Source: Jif Decadent Peanut Butter Pie, a Jif and Smucker's recipe from an old magazine ad. The steps are reworded from the original.
Most whipped topping is sold frozen, so thaw the container before you start.
The 2 tablespoons of hot fudge for the drizzle come out of the 11.75 oz jar, so you do not need a second jar.
