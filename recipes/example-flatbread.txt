Title: Example Flatbread
Cuisine: Test Kitchen
Regions: Middle East
Meal: lunch, snack
Healthy: yes
Prep: 15
Cook: 10
Servings: 6

Ingredients:
2 cups flour
3/4 cup warm water
1 tsp salt
2 tbsp olive oil
a pinch of something

Instructions:
1. Mix the flour and the salt in a bowl.
2. Pour in the water and the oil, then bring it together into a rough dough.
3. Knead it for a few minutes until it stops being sticky.
4. Rest the dough for fifteen minutes under a cloth.
5. Roll it thin, then cook each piece in a dry pan until it blisters.
