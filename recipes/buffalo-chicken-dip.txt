Title: Buffalo Chicken Dip
Cuisine: American
Regions: United States
Meal: snack
Healthy: no
Prep: 15
Cook: 33
Servings: 15

Ingredients:
For the dip:
2 lb cooked chicken breasts, chopped into small pieces
16 oz cream cheese, softened
1/4 cup sour cream
1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
1 tsp celery seed
1 tbsp garlic powder
1 tbsp onion powder
buffalo sauce to taste
salt to taste
black pepper to taste
For the topping:
1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
celery seed for sprinkling
blue cheese crumbles to taste, optional

Instructions:
1. Leave the cream cheese out to soften, ideally for about an hour before you start.
2. Chop the cooked chicken into small pieces, but do not mince it.
3. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
4. In a large bowl, mix the chicken, cream cheese, sour cream, 1 1/2 cups of cheddar, celery seed, garlic powder, onion powder, buffalo sauce, salt, and pepper until just combined. Do not overmix, because it will crush the chicken.
5. Spread the mixture into a baking dish and top it with the remaining 1/2 cup of cheddar, a little more celery seed, and blue cheese crumbles if you like.
6. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
7. Switch the oven to broil and broil for 3 minutes, until the cheese on top starts to brown.

Notes:
Allergens: milk. This is based only on the ingredients as written, so check your product labels, since rotisserie chicken seasoning, buffalo sauce, and blue cheese can contain hidden allergens such as wheat or soy, and think about cross-contact.
This makes about 15 to 20 servings.
A rotisserie chicken is the preferred choice for the chicken. You can also use chicken breasts seasoned to your preference and cooked ahead of time.
For a slow cooker version, mix the ingredients in a crock pot and cook on high for 4 hours.
