Title: Slow Cooker Thai Green Curry
Cuisine: Thai
Regions: Thailand
Meal: dinner
Healthy: yes
Prep: 15
Cook: 285
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
24 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
3 tbsp green curry paste
1 onion, sliced
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp ginger, minced
1 tbsp fish sauce
1 tbsp brown sugar
1/2 cup low sodium chicken broth
13.5 oz light coconut milk
1 zucchini, sliced
1 cup snap peas
2 cups fresh spinach
1 lime, juiced
1/4 cup Thai basil leaves

Instructions:
1. Add the chicken, curry paste, onion, garlic, ginger, fish sauce, brown sugar, and broth to the slow cooker and stir well.
2. Cook on low for 4 hours.
3. Stir in the coconut milk, zucchini, and snap peas, then cook for 45 minutes more.
4. Stir in the spinach, lime juice, and Thai basil at the end so they stay bright. The spinach wilts in about 2 minutes.

Notes:
Allergens: fish (fish sauce). Many green curry pastes also contain shrimp paste, which is crustacean shellfish, so check your label and think about cross-contact.
The green color fades after a day or two in the fridge. Stirring in a fresh handful of basil when you reheat helps.
About 320 calories per serving without rice, based on 24 oz of raw chicken thighs split into 4 servings.
For fridge storage, keep 2 portions for up to 3 to 4 days and freeze the other 2 right away.
