Title: Brown Sugar Spiced Apple Pie
Cuisine: American
Regions: United States
Meal: dessert
Healthy: no
Prep: 25
Cook: 70
Servings: 8

Ingredients:
For the filling:
3 lb Granny Smith apples, peeled, cored, and sliced 1/4 inch thick
2 tbsp butter
2/3 cup brown sugar
1/4 cup apple cider
1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
1/2 tsp ginger
1/4 tsp nutmeg
1/8 tsp clove
1/4 tsp salt
3 tbsp cornstarch
3 tbsp cold water
1 tbsp lemon juice
2 tsp vanilla extract
For the pie:
2 refrigerated pie crusts
1 egg, beaten
coarse sugar for sprinkling

Instructions:
1. Let the refrigerated crusts sit out according to the package directions, then fit one crust into a 9-inch pie pan.
2. Melt the butter in a large Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium heat. Add the apples, brown sugar, cider, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, clove, and salt.
3. Cover the pot and cook for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the apples soften and release their juices.
4. Use a slotted spoon to move the apples to a rimmed baking sheet and spread them into a single layer.
5. Bring the liquid left in the pot to a boil and cook until it reduces to about 1/2 cup, which should take 5 to 8 minutes.
6. Stir the cornstarch into the cold water until smooth, then stir it into the pot and cook for 1 minute, until the syrup is thick and glossy. Remove the pot from the heat and stir in the lemon juice and vanilla.
7. Pour the syrup over the apples and toss gently. Let the filling cool completely, which takes about an hour. Chilling it in the refrigerator will speed this up.
8. Place an oven rack in the lower third of the oven and preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil or parchment.
9. Pile the cooled filling into the bottom crust, mounding it in the center. Cover with the second crust, trim the edges, and crimp them closed. Cut 5 or 6 vents in the top.
10. Brush the top crust with the beaten egg and sprinkle it with coarse sugar.
11. Set the pie on the lined baking sheet and bake at 425°F for 20 minutes.
12. Lower the oven temperature to 375°F and bake for 30 more minutes, until the crust is deep golden and the filling bubbles through the vents. Cover the edges with foil if they brown too quickly.
13. Cool the pie on a wire rack for at least 3 hours before slicing so the filling can set.

Notes:
Allergens: wheat, milk, eggs. This is based only on the ingredients as written, so check your product labels, since refrigerated pie crusts vary by brand and can contain wheat, soy, or milk, and think about cross-contact.
This recipe makes one 9-inch pie. Make the filling twice for two pies, using one bowl or pot per batch.
Precooking the apples and boiling down their juices keeps the pie from turning watery. The crust is baked on a sheet pan to catch any bubbling over.
Follow the crust package for any oven temperature or timing differences.
