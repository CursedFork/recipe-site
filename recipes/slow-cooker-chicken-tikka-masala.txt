Title: Slow Cooker Chicken Tikka Masala
Cuisine: Indian
Regions: India
Meal: dinner
Healthy: yes
Prep: 20
Cook: 300
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
24 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into large chunks
3/4 cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
1 tbsp oil
15 oz crushed tomatoes
1 onion, finely diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp ginger, minced
1 tbsp garam masala
2 tsp ground coriander
2 tsp ground cumin
2 tsp smoked paprika
1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
1/4 cup half and half
salt to taste
1 tsp lemon juice

Instructions:
1. In the slow cooker insert, toss the chicken with 1/2 cup of the yogurt, half of the garlic, half of the ginger, and half of each spice. Let it sit while you prep the rest, or up to overnight in the fridge.
2. Add the oil, crushed tomatoes, onion, and the remaining garlic, ginger, and spices, then stir.
3. Cook on low for 5 hours.
4. Turn the heat off. Warm the remaining 1/4 cup yogurt with a ladle of the hot sauce, then stir it in with the half and half.
5. Finish with salt and the lemon juice.

Notes:
Allergens: milk (yogurt, half and half).
The chicken can cook for up to 6 hours on low.
It is spicier and more tomato-forward than the butter chicken, and the yogurt marinade gives it the tikka flavor.
About 335 calories per serving without rice, based on 24 oz of raw chicken thighs split into 4 servings.
For fridge storage, keep 2 portions for up to 3 to 4 days and freeze the other 2 right away.
