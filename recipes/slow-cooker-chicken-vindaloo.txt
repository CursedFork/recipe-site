Title: Slow Cooker Chicken Vindaloo
Cuisine: Indian
Regions: India
Meal: dinner
Healthy: yes
Prep: 15
Cook: 360
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
24 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
3 tbsp red wine vinegar
1 tbsp oil
14.5 oz diced tomatoes
2 tbsp tomato paste
1 large onion, diced
5 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp ginger, minced
1 tbsp Kashmiri chili powder
2 tsp ground cumin
2 tsp ground coriander
1 tsp mustard powder
1 tsp turmeric
1/2 tsp cinnamon
1 pinch ground clove
1 tsp sugar
salt to taste
cayenne pepper to taste
2 small potatoes, diced (optional)

Instructions:
1. Stir the vinegar and all of the spices together in the slow cooker first.
2. Add the chicken, oil, diced tomatoes, tomato paste, onion, garlic, ginger, sugar, and potatoes if using, then mix well.
3. Cook on low for 6 hours.
4. Shred the chicken lightly in the pot, then taste and adjust the heat, salt, and sugar.

Notes:
Allergens: none of the major allergens were identified from the listed ingredients.
It can also cook on high for 3 hours instead of 6 hours on low.
Paprika works in place of the Kashmiri chili powder if you cannot find it.
About 300 calories per serving without potato or rice. The optional potatoes add about 55 calories per serving.
It tastes better on day 2 or 3 as the spices settle.
For fridge storage, keep 2 portions for up to 3 to 4 days and freeze the other 2 right away.
