Title: Sample Fusion Bowl
Cuisine: Nowhere In Particular
Regions: Japan, Mexico, West Africa
Meal: lunch, dinner
Healthy: yes
Prep: 25
Cook: 0
Servings: 2

Ingredients:
1 cup cooked grain of your choosing
1/2 cup black beans
2 tbsp fictional dressing
1/4 cup shredded cabbage
1 avocado, sliced
a handful of herbs
salt to taste

Instructions:
1. Put the grain in the bottom of two bowls.
2. Pile everything else on top in whatever arrangement pleases you.
3. Spoon the dressing over, scatter the herbs and finish with salt to taste.

Notes:
A deliberately fake fusion dish, here to test a recipe that lists three regions
across three continents and has a cook time of zero. It also has no photo.
