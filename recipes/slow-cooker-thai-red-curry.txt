Title: Slow Cooker Thai Red Curry
Cuisine: Thai
Regions: Thailand
Meal: dinner
Healthy: yes
Prep: 15
Cook: 285
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
24 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
3 tbsp red curry paste
1 onion, sliced
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp ginger, minced
1 tbsp fish sauce
1 tbsp brown sugar
1/2 cup low sodium chicken broth
13.5 oz light coconut milk
2 bell peppers, sliced
1 cup green beans, trimmed
1 lime, juiced
1/4 cup Thai basil leaves

Instructions:
1. Add the chicken, curry paste, onion, garlic, ginger, fish sauce, brown sugar, and broth to the slow cooker and stir well.
2. Cook on low for 4 hours.
3. Stir in the coconut milk, bell peppers, and green beans, then cook for 45 minutes more, until the vegetables are tender but not mushy.
4. Stir in the lime juice and top with the Thai basil just before serving.

Notes:
Allergens: fish (fish sauce). Many red curry pastes also contain shrimp paste, which is crustacean shellfish, so check your label and think about cross-contact.
The vegetables can cook for up to 1 hour after the coconut milk goes in if you like them softer.
Bamboo shoots work in place of the green beans, and cilantro works in place of the Thai basil.
About 330 calories per serving without rice, based on 24 oz of raw chicken thighs split into 4 servings.
For fridge storage, keep 2 portions for up to 3 to 4 days and freeze the other 2 right away.
