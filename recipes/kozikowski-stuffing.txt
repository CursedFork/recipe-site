Title: Kozikowski Stuffing
Cuisine: American
Regions: United States
Meal: dinner
Healthy: no
Prep: 20
Cook: 65
Servings: 10

Ingredients:
1 loaf white bread, broken into pieces and dried for 3 days
3/4 cup butter
3 onions, chopped
4 ribs celery, chopped
2 tsp salt
1 tsp pepper
1 tsp poultry seasoning
3 tbsp sage, fresh and chopped
1/3 cup parsley, fresh and chopped
2 eggs
1 cup stock

Instructions:
1. Break the bread into pieces and let it dry out for 3 days.
2. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onions and celery and saute them for about 20 minutes.
3. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the salt, pepper, poultry seasoning, sage, and parsley.
4. Pour the vegetable mixture over the dried bread and toss until it is evenly mixed.
5. Whisk the eggs and stock together in a bowl, then pour the mixture over the bread and toss gently.
6. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
7. Spread the stuffing in a baking dish and cover it with foil. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
8. Remove the foil and bake for 15 more minutes.

Notes:
Allergens: wheat (gluten), milk, eggs. This is based only on the ingredients as written, so check your product labels, since bread, stock, and poultry seasoning can contain hidden sources of milk, soy, or wheat, and think about cross-contact.
This makes about 10 to 12 servings from one loaf.
White bread is the usual choice, and sourdough works well too.
The original card calls for 2 to 3 eggs, so use 3 if you want a more custard-like stuffing.
Fresh sage and parsley are ideal, but dried herbs can be used.
From a family recipe card written in November 2022.
