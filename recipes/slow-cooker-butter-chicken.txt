Title: Slow Cooker Butter Chicken
Cuisine: Indian
Regions: India
Meal: dinner
Healthy: yes
Prep: 15
Cook: 300
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
24 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, left in large pieces
15 oz tomato sauce
1 onion, grated
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp ginger, minced
1 tbsp butter
1 tbsp garam masala
2 tsp paprika
1 tsp ground cumin
1/2 tsp turmeric
1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), optional
3/4 cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
1/4 cup half and half
salt to taste
1 pinch sugar

Instructions:
1. Add the chicken, tomato sauce, onion, garlic, ginger, butter, garam masala, paprika, cumin, and turmeric to the slow cooker and stir well.
2. Cook on low for 5 hours, then shred or cut the chicken into bite-size pieces right in the pot.
3. Turn the heat off. Whisk the yogurt and half and half together in a bowl, stir in a ladle of the hot sauce to warm it, then stir the mixture into the pot.
4. Taste and add salt, the pinch of sugar, and the fenugreek leaves if using.

Notes:
Allergens: milk (butter, yogurt, half and half).
The chicken can cook for up to 6 hours on low.
Adding the yogurt off the heat, after warming it slightly, keeps it from curdling.
About 330 calories per serving without rice, based on 24 oz of raw chicken thighs split into 4 servings.
For fridge storage, keep 2 portions for up to 3 to 4 days and freeze the other 2 right away.
