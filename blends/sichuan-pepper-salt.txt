Name: Sichuan Pepper Salt
Aka: hua jiao yan, huajiao salt, Szechuan pepper salt, jiaoyan
Cuisines: Sichuan, Chinese
Origin: China
Uses: Table seasoning, Finishing sprinkle
Methods: Raw or finishing
Classic: Chicken, Duck, Pork
Also: Pork ribs, Fish
Tip: Toast the Sichuan pepper and salt together in a dry pan until fragrant, then grind. Serve it on the side for fried or roasted meat.
Typical version: The page says it is a mixture of salt and Sichuan pepper, toasted and browned in a wok, and served as a condiment with chicken, duck and pork dishes.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan_pepper
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page confirms the two ingredients and the toasting. The ratio is a common one that I chose.

Ingredients:
1 part Pepper, Sichuan
2 parts Salt
