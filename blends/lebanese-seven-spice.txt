Name: Lebanese Seven Spice
Aka: sabaa baharat, seven spice, Lebanese 7 spice, Lebanese 7-spice
Cuisines: Lebanese, Levantine
Origin: Lebanon
Uses: Dry rub, Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Roast, Braise or stew, Grill
Classic: Ground lamb, Beef, Chicken
Also: Stews and braises, Rice, Chickpeas
Tip: Use about a teaspoon per pound of ground meat, in kofta, meatballs and fillings, or as a rub for beef and chicken.
Typical version: Allspice and cinnamon lead, with coriander, black pepper, cumin, clove and nutmeg.
Source: https://www.alphafoodie.com/lebanese-7-spice-blend-sabaa-baharat/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Alphafoodie), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is half a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. Some Lebanese cooks use different seven spices, and this page differs from a Gulf-style baharat.

Ingredients:
12 parts Allspice
12 parts Cinnamon
9 parts Coriander
6 parts Pepper, black
4 parts Cumin
2 parts Clove
2 parts Nutmeg
