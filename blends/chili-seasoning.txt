Name: Chili Seasoning
Aka: chili mix, chili spice mix, chili seasoning mix
Cuisines: American, Tex-Mex
Origin: United States
Uses: Cooking base or sauce, Dry rub
Methods: Simmer or boil, Braise or stew, Slow cook
Classic: Chili, Ground beef, Beans and lentils
Also: Soup, Stews and braises, Corn
Tip: Start with 2 tablespoons per pound of meat and stir it in as the meat browns. It replaces a store-bought chili packet.
Typical version: A chili powder base with cumin, garlic, onion, paprika, oregano, salt and pepper.
Source: https://crayonsandcravings.com/homemade-chili-seasoning/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Crayons and Cravings), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is half a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. Chili powder is itself a blend, so the mild red chile stands in for it here.

Ingredients:
30 parts Chile, mild red
12 parts Cumin
12 parts Garlic powder
12 parts Onion powder
12 parts Paprika
6 parts Oregano
3 parts Salt
3 parts Pepper, black
