Name: Greek Seasoning
Aka: Greek spice mix, Mediterranean seasoning, Greek herb mix
Cuisines: Greek, American
Origin: Greece
Uses: Dry rub, Table seasoning
Methods: Grill, Roast, Bake
Classic: Chicken, Lamb, Vegetables
Also: Salad dressings, Potatoes, Fish
Tip: Use it in marinades with olive oil and lemon, on kebabs, or sprinkled over roasted potatoes and salads.
Typical version: Oregano leads, with basil, garlic and onion powder, dill, thyme, rosemary and parsley, plus salt, pepper and a trace of nutmeg.
Source: https://www.tasteofhome.com/recipes/easy-greek-seasoning/print/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Taste of Home), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is an eighth of a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. This is an American idea of Greek flavors, not a traditional Greek blend.

Ingredients:
20 parts Oregano
12 parts Basil
12 parts Garlic powder
8 parts Onion powder
6 parts Dill
4 parts Salt
4 parts Thyme
4 parts Rosemary
4 parts Parsley
2 parts Pepper, black
1 part Nutmeg
