Name: Thai Curry Powder
Aka: Thai yellow curry powder, Thai-style curry powder
Cuisines: Thai
Origin: Thailand
Uses: Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Simmer or boil, Sauté or stir-fry
Classic: Curry, Chicken, Shrimp
Also: Tofu, Potatoes, Rice
Tip: Add 1 to 2 tablespoons to sauces and curries, or a bit to boost a curry paste. It is sweeter and more citrusy than Indian curry powder.
Typical version: Coriander and cumin lead, with turmeric, ginger, red chile flakes, bay leaf and a touch of clove.
Source: https://veganwithgusto.com/thai-curry-powder/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Vegan with Gusto), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. Thai curries are normally made from wet pastes, so this dry powder is a home shortcut. The recipe counts bay leaves and cloves, which I turned into parts by estimate.

Ingredients:
9 parts Coriander
6 parts Cumin
3 parts Turmeric
3 parts Chile flakes
2 parts Ginger
1 part Bay leaf
1 part Clove
