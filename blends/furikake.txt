Name: Furikake
Aka: rice seasoning, rice topping
Cuisines: Japanese
Origin: Japan
Uses: Table seasoning, Finishing sprinkle
Methods: Raw or finishing
Classic: Rice, Fish
Also: Scrambled and fried eggs, Popcorn, Noodles
Tip: Shake it over hot rice, eggs, popcorn or noodles. Nori and sesame make this version vegetarian, though many commercial ones contain dried fish.
Typical version: A dry-ingredients version of nori, sesame, salt and a little sugar. Shop furikake often adds dried fish, egg or shiso.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furikake
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page lists nori, dried fish, roe, sesame, shiso and salt. This version leaves out the animal and shiso ingredients, and the parts are mine.

Ingredients:
4 parts Nori
4 parts Sesame seed
1 part Salt
1 part Sugar
