Name: Seasoned Salt
Aka: season salt, all-purpose seasoned salt
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Table seasoning, Dry rub
Methods: Grill, Roast, Fry, Raw or finishing
Classic: Potatoes, Chicken, Ground beef
Also: Steak, Popcorn, Eggs
Tip: Sprinkle on fries, burgers and chicken. It already has salt, so skip the extra.
Typical version: The page says seasoned salt is table salt blended with herbs and spices. This version is mostly salt, with paprika, onion, garlic and turmeric for color.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seasoned_salt
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page describes the idea and not a recipe. The spice choices and every part are my own typical version.

Ingredients:
8 parts Salt
2 parts Paprika
1 part Onion powder
1 part Garlic powder
1/2 part Turmeric
1/2 part Pepper, black
1/2 part Sugar
