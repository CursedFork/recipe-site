Name: Pizza Seasoning
Aka: pizza spice, pizza herb blend
Cuisines: Italian, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Table seasoning, Dry rub
Methods: Bake, Raw or finishing
Classic: Pizza, Tomato sauces, Bread and toast
Also: Pasta, Popcorn, Dips and spreads
Tip: Stir it into pizza sauce, sprinkle it on before or after baking, or use it on garlic bread, popcorn and meat.
Typical version: Oregano leads, with basil, onion, garlic, thyme, fennel, paprika and red pepper flakes.
Source: https://dizzybusyandhungry.com/pizza-seasoning/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Dizzy, Busy and Hungry), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. The recipe uses onion flakes, and onion powder stands in here. It is closer to American pizzeria style than to anything Italian.

Ingredients:
4 parts Oregano
2 parts Basil
1 part Onion powder
1 part Garlic powder
1 part Thyme
1 part Fennel seed
1 part Paprika
1 part Chile flakes
