Name: Tabil
Aka: tabil spice, Tunisian spice mix, tābil
Cuisines: Tunisian
Origin: Tunisia
Uses: Cooking base or sauce, Dry rub
Methods: Braise or stew, Roast, Simmer or boil
Classic: Soup, Stews and braises, Lamb
Also: Chickpeas, Fish, Eggs
Tip: Toast the coriander and caraway seeds, grind them, then stir into soups, stews and harissa. Garlic is part of the mix.
Typical version: Coriander seed, caraway seed, garlic powder and chile powder, as the page lists. Cumin is a common extra.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabil
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The ingredients come from the source, but the source gives no amounts. The parts are a typical home ratio that I chose, so treat them as a starting point to taste and adjust.

Ingredients:
4 parts Coriander
3 parts Caraway
2 parts Garlic powder
2 parts Chile, hot red
1 part Cumin (optional)
