Name: Ranch Seasoning
Aka: ranch seasoning mix, ranch dressing mix, ranch powder
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Table seasoning, Dry rub
Methods: Raw or finishing, Roast, Bake
Classic: Salad dressings, Dips and spreads, Chicken
Also: Potatoes, Popcorn, Chips and crackers
Tip: Stir 3 tablespoons into mayonnaise and milk for dressing, or into sour cream for a dip. It is also good on popcorn, potatoes and chicken.
Typical version: Parsley, onion, garlic, dill and chives with salt and pepper. The source also adds dry buttermilk powder, which is not a spice and is left out here.
Source: https://crayonsandcravings.com/ranch-seasoning/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Crayons and Cravings), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a quarter teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. The recipe also has 1/3 cup dry buttermilk powder, which I left out. Without it this is the herb part of ranch only, and buttermilk powder is a milk ingredient.

Ingredients:
12 parts Parsley
9 parts Onion powder
6 parts Garlic powder
6 parts Dill
4 parts Chives
2 parts Salt
1 part Pepper, black
