Name: Fajita Seasoning
Aka: fajita spice, fajita mix
Cuisines: Tex-Mex, Mexican, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub
Methods: Grill, Sauté or stir-fry
Classic: Chicken, Steak, Bell peppers
Also: Shrimp, Onions and leeks, fajitas
Tip: Mix about 1 1/2 tablespoons with a pound of meat and cook it with sliced onions and peppers on a hot skillet or grill.
Typical version: Chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic and onion, a little sugar, salt, black pepper and cayenne.
Source: https://www.spendwithpennies.com/fajita-seasoning/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Spend with Pennies), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a quarter teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. Chili powder is itself a blend, so the mild red chile stands in for it here.

Ingredients:
12 parts Chile, mild red
8 parts Cumin
8 parts Paprika: Smoked
8 parts Garlic powder
4 parts Onion powder
4 parts Sugar (optional)
2 parts Salt
2 parts Pepper, black
1 part Chile, hot red: Cayenne
