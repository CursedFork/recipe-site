Name: Chai Masala
Aka: masala chai spice, tea masala, chai spice
Cuisines: Indian, Pakistani
Origin: India
Uses: Drink or infusion
Methods: Simmer or boil
Classic: Tea and chai
Also: Milk and cream, Cakes and muffins, Oats and porridge
Tip: Simmer a spoonful in water and milk with black tea, then strain. Use whole spices lightly crushed if you have them.
Typical version: Cardamom and cinnamon lead, with ginger, black pepper and clove. Star anise and nutmeg are optional extras that many families add.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masala_chai
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says masala chai is tea, milk, spices and sugar, and I did not find a standard spice list. This ratio is a typical home version that I chose.

Ingredients:
4 parts Cardamom
4 parts Cinnamon
3 parts Ginger
2 parts Pepper, black
1 part Clove
1 part Star anise (optional)
1/2 part Nutmeg (optional)
