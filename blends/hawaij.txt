Name: Hawaij
Aka: hawayej, hawaij for soup, Yemeni soup spice
Cuisines: Yemeni, Israeli
Origin: Yemen
Uses: Cooking base or sauce, Dry rub
Methods: Simmer or boil, Braise or stew
Classic: Soup, Chicken, Stews and braises
Also: Rice, Lentils, Lamb
Tip: Stir it into soups and broths, or rub it onto chicken and lamb before cooking. This is the savory version, and the coffee version is different.
Typical version: Cumin, black pepper, turmeric and cardamom, as the page describes it.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaij
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The four spices are from the page. The parts are my typical ratio, and Yemeni cooks differ.

Ingredients:
4 parts Cumin
4 parts Pepper, black
3 parts Turmeric
1 part Cardamom
