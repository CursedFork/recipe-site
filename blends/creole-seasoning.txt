Name: Creole Seasoning
Aka: Creole spice, Creole spice mix, Creole rub
Cuisines: Cajun and Creole, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub, Table seasoning
Methods: Grill, Roast, Sauté or stir-fry, Simmer or boil
Classic: Shrimp, Chicken, Rice
Also: Sausage, Soup, Eggs
Tip: Sprinkle it on shrimp, chicken, gumbo, rice and eggs. It is a bit more herb-heavy than Cajun seasoning.
Typical version: Paprika leads, with cayenne, basil, garlic, onion, oregano, thyme, black and white pepper, celery seed and a little salt.
Source: https://pepperscale.com/homemade-creole-seasoning/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Pepperscale), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a tablespoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. The recipe is light on salt, as it says itself.

Ingredients:
4 parts Paprika
2 parts Chile, hot red: Cayenne
2 parts Basil
2 parts Garlic powder
2 parts Onion powder
2 parts Oregano
1 part Salt
1 part Thyme
1 part Pepper, black
1 part Pepper, white
1 part Celery seed
