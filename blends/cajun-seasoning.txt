Name: Cajun Seasoning
Aka: Cajun spice, Cajun spice mix, Cajun rub
Cuisines: Cajun and Creole, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub, Table seasoning
Methods: Grill, Roast, Sauté or stir-fry, Fry
Classic: Chicken, Shrimp, White fish
Also: Potatoes, Rice, Sausage
Tip: Use about 1 tablespoon per pound of meat. It suits chicken, shrimp, fish, potatoes and rice, and it is hot, so adjust the cayenne to taste.
Typical version: Paprika leads, with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, oregano, thyme, black pepper and cayenne, and red pepper flakes if you want more heat.
Source: https://www.spendwithpennies.com/cajun-seasoning/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Spend with Pennies), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is half a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. One author I read argues that thyme and oregano are not in a true Cajun blend, so purists may disagree.

Ingredients:
12 parts Paprika
6 parts Garlic powder
4 parts Onion powder
3 parts Salt
3 parts Oregano
3 parts Thyme
2 parts Pepper, black
2 parts Chile, hot red: Cayenne
1 part Chile flakes (optional)
