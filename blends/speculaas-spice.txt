Name: Speculaas Spice
Aka: speculoos spice, speculaaskruiden, Dutch windmill cookie spice
Cuisines: Dutch
Origin: Netherlands
Uses: Sweet or baking
Methods: Bake
Classic: Cookies, Cakes and muffins
Also: Coffee and hot chocolate, Oats and porridge
Tip: Use it in cookie dough, cakes and cookie butter, and in warm drinks. Cinnamon is the base and the others are in small amounts.
Typical version: Cinnamon with cloves, nutmeg, cardamom and white pepper, and sometimes ginger, mace or coriander.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speculaas
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page lists the cookie spices as cloves, nutmeg, cardamom and white pepper, and sometimes mace, ginger and coriander, plus cinnamon in the dough. The parts are my typical ratio.

Ingredients:
8 parts Cinnamon
2 parts Ginger
1 part Clove
1 part Nutmeg
1 part Cardamom
1 part Pepper, white
1/2 part Coriander (optional)
1/2 part Mace (optional)
