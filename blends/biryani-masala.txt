Name: Biryani Masala
Aka: biryani spice, biryani masala powder
Cuisines: Indian, Pakistani
Origin: India
Uses: Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Simmer or boil, Braise or stew
Classic: Pilaf and biryani, Chicken, Goat meat
Also: Lamb, Rice, Vegetables
Tip: Layer it with the meat, rice and fried onions before steaming. It is a warm, floral mix with no heat of its own beyond the chile.
Typical version: Coriander and cumin lead, with caraway, black pepper, fennel, cinnamon, nutmeg and mace, clove, black and green cardamom, star anise, bay leaf, Kashmiri chile and turmeric.
Source: https://hebbarskitchen.com/homemade-biryani-masala-powder-recipe/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Hebbar's Kitchen), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. The recipe counts whole pieces such as chiles, bay leaves and mace blades, which I turned into parts by estimate.

Ingredients:
6 parts Coriander
3 parts Cumin
3 parts Caraway
3 parts Pepper, black
3 parts Cardamom
2 parts Cinnamon
2 parts Black cardamom
2 parts Star anise
2 parts Bay leaf
2 parts Chile, mild red: Kashmiri
1 part Fennel seed
1 part Nutmeg
1 part Mace
1 part Clove
1 part Turmeric
