Name: Steak Seasoning
Aka: steak rub, homemade steak seasoning
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub
Methods: Grill, Fry, Roast
Classic: Steak, Roast beef, Ground beef
Also: Pork chops, Lamb chops, Mushrooms
Tip: Use about a tablespoon per pound of meat. Press it on both sides, rest the steak 15 to 20 minutes, then cook.
Typical version: Salt and black pepper, with garlic, onion, rosemary and paprika.
Source: https://www.alphafoodie.com/steak-seasoning-recipe/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Alphafoodie), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a quarter teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ.

Ingredients:
12 parts Salt
6 parts Pepper, black
6 parts Garlic powder
3 parts Onion powder
3 parts Rosemary
3 parts Paprika
