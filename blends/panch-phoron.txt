Name: Panch Phoron
Aka: panch phoran, Bengali five spice, five-spice mix (Bengali)
Cuisines: Indian, Bangladeshi
Origin: Bangladesh, India
Uses: Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Sauté or stir-fry
Classic: Lentils, Potatoes
Also: Eggplant, Cauliflower and broccoli, Green beans and peas
Tip: Use the seeds whole, never ground. Fry them in hot oil for a few seconds until they crackle, then add the vegetables or lentils.
Typical version: Equal parts of five whole seeds: fenugreek, nigella, black mustard, cumin and fennel. The page notes that wild celery seed (radhuni) can stand in for the mustard.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panch_phoron
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The five seeds and the equal parts are from the page. The pairings come from general practice in Bengali cooking, and I did not find them listed on a page.

Ingredients:
1 part Fenugreek
1 part Nigella
1 part Mustard seed
1 part Cumin
1 part Fennel seed
