Name: Cinnamon Sugar
Aka: cinnamon and sugar
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Sweet or baking
Methods: Raw or finishing, Bake
Classic: Cakes and muffins, Sweet breads and buns, Oats and porridge
Also: Apples and pears, Bread and toast, Cookies
Tip: Dust it on buttered toast, snickerdoodles, churros or oatmeal. Make a jar and shake it on.
Typical version: Ground cinnamon and granulated sugar, often about one part cinnamon to four parts sugar.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinnamon_sugar
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page confirms cinnamon and granulated sugar. The ratio is a common home one that I could not source.

Ingredients:
1 part Cinnamon
4 parts Sugar
