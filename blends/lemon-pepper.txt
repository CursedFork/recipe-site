Name: Lemon Pepper
Aka: lemon pepper seasoning, lemon-pepper
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub, Table seasoning
Methods: Grill, Roast, Bake, Fry
Classic: Chicken, Fish, Shrimp
Also: Pasta, Green beans and peas, Popcorn
Tip: Sprinkle it on chicken wings, fish and vegetables before or after cooking. Shop versions are saltier, so taste first.
Typical version: Dried lemon peel crushed with black pepper, and often salt.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemon_pepper
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says it is made from granulated lemon zest and cracked black peppercorns. Salt is common in shop versions, so I listed it as optional. The parts are mine.

Ingredients:
3 parts Lemon peel
2 parts Pepper, black
1 part Salt (optional)
