Name: Adobo Seasoning
Aka: adobo, dry adobo, adobo seco, all-purpose seasoning
Cuisines: Latin American, Caribbean, Filipino
Origin: Spain
Uses: Dry rub, Table seasoning
Methods: Grill, Roast, Sauté or stir-fry
Classic: Chicken, Pork, Beef
Also: Rice, Beans and lentils, Fish
Tip: Shake it on meat and rice. Because it is salty, lightly cut the salt in the recipe.
Typical version: The page describes adobo as paprika, oregano, salt, garlic and vinegar. The vinegar is left out of this dry version.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adobo
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page covers adobo as a wet marinade from Spain, with different forms in the Philippines and Latin America. The dry shaker mix is a Latin American product style, and the parts are mine.

Ingredients:
2 parts Paprika
2 parts Oregano
2 parts Garlic powder
2 parts Salt
1 part Pepper, black (optional)
1 part Cumin (optional)
