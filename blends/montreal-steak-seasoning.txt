Name: Montreal Steak Seasoning
Aka: Montreal seasoning, steak spice
Cuisines: American
Origin: Canada
Uses: Dry rub
Methods: Grill, Roast, Fry
Classic: Steak, Ground beef
Also: Pork chops, Roast beef, Potatoes
Tip: Press it onto steak a few minutes before cooking. It is salty and coarse, so go lighter on extra salt.
Typical version: Garlic, coriander, black pepper, cayenne pepper flakes, dill seed and salt, as the page describes.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal_steak_seasoning
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The ingredients come from the source, but the source gives no amounts. The parts are a typical home ratio that I chose, so treat them as a starting point to taste and adjust.

Ingredients:
3 parts Garlic powder
3 parts Coriander
3 parts Pepper, black
2 parts Chile flakes
1 part Dill
4 parts Salt
