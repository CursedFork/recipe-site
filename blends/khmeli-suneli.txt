Name: Khmeli Suneli
Aka: khmeli-suneli, Georgian spice mix, hops suneli
Cuisines: Georgian
Origin: Georgia
Uses: Cooking base or sauce, Dry rub
Methods: Braise or stew, Simmer or boil, Roast
Classic: Chicken, Beans and lentils, Stews and braises
Also: Lamb, Tomatoes, Eggplant
Tip: Add it to stews, bean dishes and chicken, or stir it into walnut sauces. It is an herb-heavy mix with a fenugreek note.
Typical version: The page lists coriander seed, celery seed, basil, dill, parsley, blue fenugreek, summer savory, bay leaf, mint and marigold.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmeli_suneli
Confidence: low
Doubt: The ingredient list is from the page. Marigold is left out because it is not in this library, and I used ordinary fenugreek for blue fenugreek. The parts are my guess, and Georgian cooks vary.

Ingredients:
3 parts Coriander
2 parts Basil
2 parts Savory
1 part Fenugreek
1 part Dill
1 part Parsley
1 part Mint
1 part Bay leaf
1 part Celery seed (optional)
