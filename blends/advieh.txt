Name: Advieh
Aka: advieh spice, Persian spice mix, advieh-e khoresh
Cuisines: Persian
Origin: Iran
Uses: Cooking base or sauce, Dry rub
Methods: Braise or stew, Simmer or boil
Classic: Rice, Chicken, Stews and braises
Also: Lamb, Pilaf and biryani, Lentils
Tip: Stir into rice dishes and stews near the end of cooking. The rose petals are what make this taste Persian.
Typical version: Turmeric, cinnamon, cardamom, clove, rose petals, cumin and ginger, as the page names them.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advieh
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says the mix varies from the Persian Gulf to the Caspian Sea. The parts and the choice of these spices are my typical version.

Ingredients:
2 parts Cinnamon
2 parts Cardamom
2 parts Cumin
2 parts Rose petals
1 part Turmeric
1 part Ginger
1/2 part Clove
