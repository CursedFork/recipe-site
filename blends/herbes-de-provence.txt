Name: Herbes de Provence
Aka: Provencal herbs, herbs of Provence
Cuisines: French
Origin: France
Uses: Dry rub, Table seasoning
Methods: Roast, Grill, Bake
Classic: Chicken, Lamb, Potatoes
Also: Tomatoes, Eggplant, Fish, Bread and toast
Tip: Crumble it over chicken, lamb, roasted vegetables and potatoes before cooking, so the herbs release their oils.
Typical version: Thyme and savory lead, with rosemary, marjoram and oregano. Basil and tarragon are optional, and some modern blends add lavender.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbes_de_Provence
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says these blends often contain savory, marjoram, rosemary, thyme, oregano, tarragon, basil and black pepper. The parts are my typical ratio.

Ingredients:
3 parts Thyme
2 parts Savory
2 parts Rosemary
1 part Marjoram
1 part Oregano
1 part Basil (optional)
1 part Tarragon (optional)
