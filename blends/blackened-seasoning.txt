Name: Blackened Seasoning
Aka: blackening seasoning, blackening spice, blackened spice
Cuisines: Cajun and Creole, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub
Methods: Sauté or stir-fry, Grill, Fry
Classic: White fish, Chicken, Shrimp
Also: Salmon, Steak, Vegetables
Tip: Coat the food generously, then sear it in a very hot cast iron skillet with butter. The spices darken but should not burn, and the kitchen will smoke.
Typical version: Paprika leads, with cayenne, onion, garlic, salt, black pepper, thyme, basil and oregano.
Source: https://www.saltandlavender.com/homemade-blackened-seasoning/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Salt and Lavender), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is half a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ.

Ingredients:
12 parts Paprika
2 parts Chile, hot red: Cayenne
2 parts Onion powder
2 parts Garlic powder
2 parts Salt
1 part Pepper, black
1 part Thyme
1 part Basil
1 part Oregano
