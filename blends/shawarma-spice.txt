Name: Shawarma Spice
Aka: shawarma seasoning, shawarma spice mix
Cuisines: Levantine, Lebanese, Turkish
Origin: Levant
Uses: Wet marinade or paste, Dry rub
Methods: Grill, Roast
Classic: Chicken, Lamb, Beef
Also: Cauliflower and broccoli, Flatbread, Tofu
Tip: Mix with oil, lemon juice and yogurt, marinate overnight, then roast or grill thin cuts.
Typical version: The page says shawarma meat marinates in coriander, cumin, cardamom, paprika and garlic, and may use cinnamon, turmeric and sometimes baharat.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawarma
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The ingredients come from the source, but the source gives no amounts. The parts are a typical home ratio that I chose, so treat them as a starting point to taste and adjust.

Ingredients:
4 parts Cumin
3 parts Paprika
2 parts Coriander
2 parts Garlic powder
1 part Turmeric
1 part Cinnamon
1 part Cardamom
