Name: Pickling Spice
Aka: pickling spices, pickle spice, brining spice
Cuisines: American, German, Eastern European
Origin: United States
Uses: Pickling or brining
Methods: Simmer or boil
Classic: Vegetables, Cabbage and Brussels sprouts
Also: Carrots, Beets, Pork
Tip: Use 1/2 to 1 tablespoon per quart of vegetables. Crush the bay leaves and clove first, and tie the spices in a cloth bag if you want them out of the jar.
Typical version: Mustard seed leads, with coriander, black pepper, dill seed, allspice, red pepper flakes, clove, bay leaf and a little ginger.
Source: https://leelalicious.com/wprm_print/homemade-pickling-spice-recipe
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Leelalicious), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. The recipe counts whole cloves and bay leaves, which I turned into parts by estimate.

Ingredients:
6 parts Mustard seed
3 parts Coriander
3 parts Pepper, black
3 parts Dill (optional)
1 part Allspice
1 part Chile flakes
1 part Clove
2 parts Bay leaf
1 part Ginger (optional)
