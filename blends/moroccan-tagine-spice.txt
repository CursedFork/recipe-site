Name: Moroccan Tagine Spice
Aka: tagine spice, tagine seasoning, Moroccan spice blend
Cuisines: Moroccan
Origin: Morocco
Uses: Cooking base or sauce, Dry rub
Methods: Braise or stew, Slow cook, Roast
Classic: Chicken, Lamb, Stews and braises
Also: Chickpeas, Carrots, Rice
Tip: Rub it on chicken or lamb before roasting, or stir it into the tagine as you brown the meat. It is fragrant but mild.
Typical version: Paprika and cumin with cinnamon, coriander, cardamom, garlic, allspice and salt.
Source: https://thishealthytable.com/blog/tagine-spice/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (This Healthy Table), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a quarter teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. Ras el hanout is the more traditional Moroccan blend, and this page is a simpler home-cook version.

Ingredients:
4 parts Paprika
2 parts Salt
2 parts Cumin
1 part Cinnamon
1 part Coriander
1 part Cardamom
1 part Garlic powder
1 part Allspice
