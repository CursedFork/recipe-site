Name: Garam Masala
Aka: garam masala powder
Cuisines: Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Nepali
Origin: Indian Subcontinent
Uses: Finishing sprinkle, Wet marinade or paste, Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Sauté or stir-fry, Braise or stew
Classic: Lamb, Chicken, Lentils
Also: Cauliflower, Potatoes, Eggs
Tip: Stir it in near the end of cooking so the aroma stays strong, or rub it into meat before cooking.
Typical version: One ground-spice version, with the amounts converted to parts by volume (one part is about 2 teaspoons) and cinnamon rounded from 2 1/4 parts down to 2. Garam masala has no single standard. Another tested recipe I read is mostly black pepper and cardamom and leaves out the clove, and cooks also add star anise, mace, fennel or bay.

Ingredients:
8 parts Coriander
6 parts Cumin
3 parts Pepper, black
3 parts Cardamom
2 parts Cinnamon
1 part Clove
1 part Ginger (optional)
1/2 part Nutmeg (optional)
