Name: Mitmita
Aka: mitmita spice, Ethiopian chile salt
Cuisines: Ethiopian
Origin: Ethiopia
Uses: Table seasoning, Dry rub
Methods: Raw or finishing, Grill
Classic: Beef, Eggs
Also: Lamb, Steak
Tip: Used as a hot table seasoning, and a pinch goes on raw or lightly cooked beef. It is very hot.
Typical version: Ground African bird's eye chile, Ethiopian cardamom (korarima), clove and salt, as the page lists. Ordinary cardamom stands in for korarima here.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitmita
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page lists the four ingredients. Korarima is a different spice from the ordinary green cardamom used here, and the parts are mine.

Ingredients:
8 parts Chile, hot red
1 part Cardamom
1/2 part Clove
2 parts Salt
