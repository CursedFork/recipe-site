Name: Berbere
Aka: berbere spice, Ethiopian red spice, berberé
Cuisines: Ethiopian, East African
Origin: Ethiopia
Uses: Cooking base or sauce, Dry rub
Methods: Braise or stew, Sauté or stir-fry
Classic: Chicken, Lentils, Stews and braises
Also: Beef, Lamb, Eggs
Tip: Fry it in oil or clarified butter before adding liquid. Berbere is hot and deep, so start with less than a recipe calls for.
Typical version: Mostly dried red chile, with coriander, ginger, garlic, ajwain, nigella and fenugreek. Many versions also use korarima, rue and besobela, which are not in this library.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berbere
Confidence: medium
