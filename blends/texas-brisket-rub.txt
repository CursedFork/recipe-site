Name: Texas Brisket Rub
Aka: brisket rub, Texas rub
Cuisines: American BBQ, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub
Methods: Smoke, Roast, Slow cook
Classic: Brisket and beef ribs, Roast beef, Pork ribs
Also: Steak, Pulled pork
Tip: Use about 1 to 1 1/2 tablespoons per pound. Press it in on every side, then let the meat rest 30 to 45 minutes before smoking.
Typical version: Plenty of salt, smoked paprika, garlic powder and black pepper, with chili powder, cumin and cayenne.
Source: https://stateofdinner.com/texas-brisket-rub/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (State of Dinner), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. True Texas rubs are often just salt and pepper, and this one is a spiced, modern version. Chili powder is itself a blend, so the mild red chile stands in for it here.

Ingredients:
9 parts Salt
6 parts Paprika: Smoked
6 parts Garlic powder
6 parts Pepper, black
3 parts Chile, mild red
2 parts Cumin
2 parts Chile, hot red: Cayenne
