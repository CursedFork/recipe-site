Name: Fines Herbes
Aka: fine herbs
Cuisines: French
Origin: France
Uses: Finishing sprinkle, Table seasoning
Methods: Raw or finishing, Sauté or stir-fry
Classic: Scrambled and fried eggs, Omelets and frittatas, Chicken
Also: White fish, Mayonnaise and creamy sauces, Salad dressings
Tip: Fold in at the end of cooking, because the delicate herbs lose their flavor in heat. Fresh is far better than dried.
Typical version: The page says the canonical fines herbes are parsley, chives, tarragon and chervil, in roughly equal measure.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fines_herbes
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The four herbs are from the page. The dried form is much weaker than fresh herbs, which is how this blend is normally used, so the Power ratings here are low.

Ingredients:
1 part Parsley
1 part Chives
1 part Tarragon
1 part Chervil
