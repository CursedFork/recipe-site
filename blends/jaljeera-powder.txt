Name: Jaljeera Powder
Aka: jal jeera, jaljira, jaljeera masala
Cuisines: Indian
Origin: India
Uses: Drink or infusion, Table seasoning
Methods: Raw or finishing
Classic: Syrups and cocktails
Also: Snack mixes, Yogurt
Tip: Stir a spoonful into cold water or buttermilk, with a squeeze of lemon, for a salty, sour summer drink.
Typical version: Cumin, ginger, black pepper, mint, black salt and a sour fruit powder, with a little chile. The page lists these as jaljira spices.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaljeera
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The ingredients come from the source, but the source gives no amounts. The parts are a typical home ratio that I chose, so treat them as a starting point to taste and adjust.

Ingredients:
4 parts Cumin
2 parts Ginger
2 parts Black salt
2 parts Mint
2 parts Amchur
1 part Pepper, black
1/2 part Chile, hot red (optional)
