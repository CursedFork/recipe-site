Name: Bouquet Garni
Aka: herb bundle, bouquet of herbs
Cuisines: French
Origin: France
Uses: Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Simmer or boil, Braise or stew, Slow cook
Classic: Broth and stock, Soup, Stews and braises
Also: Beans and lentils, Tomato sauces, Gravy and pan sauces
Tip: Tie the herbs in a bundle or a cloth bag so you can lift it out. In dry form, use a little muslin or a tea ball.
Typical version: The page says there is no standard recipe, but most French ones include thyme, bay leaf and parsley.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bouquet_garni
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The three core herbs are from the page. It is normally a bundle of fresh herbs, so the dried form is a convenience. The parts are mine.
