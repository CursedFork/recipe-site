Name: Gingerbread Spice
Aka: gingerbread spice mix, ginger cookie spice, ginger snap spice
Cuisines: American, British, German, Scandinavian
Origin: United Kingdom
Uses: Sweet or baking
Methods: Bake
Classic: Cookies, Cakes and muffins
Also: Sweet breads and buns, Oats and porridge
Tip: Use it in ginger cookies, gingerbread and spice cakes. Ginger leads, with cinnamon and clove behind it.
Typical version: Mostly ginger, with cinnamon, clove and nutmeg. The page says gingersnaps use powdered ginger with cinnamon and clove most commonly.

Doubt: The pages describe the cookies, not a bottled spice mix. The ingredient list and parts are a typical home mix that I chose.

Ingredients:
4 parts Ginger
3 parts Cinnamon
1 part Clove
1 part Nutmeg
1 part Allspice (optional)
