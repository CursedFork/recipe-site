Name: Baharat
Aka: Arabic seven spice, Lebanese seven spice, bahar
Cuisines: Gulf Arab, Iraqi, Levantine, Turkish
Origin: Middle East
Uses: Dry rub, Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Roast, Braise or stew, Grill
Classic: Lamb, Chicken, Rice
Also: Beef, Soup, Fish
Tip: Rub it into lamb or chicken before cooking, or stir a spoonful into rice, soups and tomato sauces.
Typical version: A Gulf-style mix of black pepper, paprika, cumin and coriander with cinnamon, clove, cardamom and nutmeg. The page says the composition depends on the region.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baharat
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says the composition depends on the region, so there is no standard. This ratio is a typical version that I chose.

Ingredients:
4 parts Pepper, black
3 parts Paprika
3 parts Cumin
2 parts Coriander
2 parts Cinnamon
1 part Cardamom
1 part Nutmeg
1 part Clove
