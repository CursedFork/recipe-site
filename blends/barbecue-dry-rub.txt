Name: Barbecue Dry Rub
Aka: BBQ rub, basic dry rub, barbecue rub, spice rub
Cuisines: American BBQ, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub
Methods: Smoke, Grill, Roast, Slow cook
Classic: Pork ribs, Pulled pork, Brisket and beef ribs
Also: Chicken, Pork chops, Roast beef
Tip: Press it onto meat and rest it for an hour or overnight. The sugar caramelizes, so cook low and slow to avoid burning.
Typical version: The page says a basic rub has brown sugar, paprika, black pepper, chili powder, garlic powder, onion powder and salt. White sugar is used here because brown sugar is not in this library.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_rub
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The ingredients come from the source, but the source gives no amounts. The parts are a typical home ratio that I chose, so treat them as a starting point to taste and adjust.

Ingredients:
4 parts Sugar
4 parts Paprika
2 parts Salt
1 part Pepper, black
1 part Chile, mild red
1 part Garlic powder
1 part Onion powder
