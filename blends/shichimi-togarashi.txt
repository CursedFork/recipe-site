Name: Shichimi Togarashi
Aka: shichimi, nanami togarashi, Japanese seven-spice, seven flavor chili pepper
Cuisines: Japanese
Origin: Japan
Uses: Table seasoning, Finishing sprinkle
Methods: Raw or finishing
Classic: Noodles, Soup
Also: Rice, Tofu, Chicken
Tip: Shake it on at the table over noodles, soup, rice or grilled meat. It is mostly for finishing and does not stand up to long cooking.
Typical version: Seven parts in the name: chile, sansho or Sichuan pepper, orange peel, sesame, poppy seed, nori and ginger. Hemp seed and others are sometimes swapped in.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shichimi
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says shichimi contains seven ingredients. Japanese sansho is replaced here by Sichuan pepper, which is a different plant with a similar tingle. The parts are my typical ratio.

Ingredients:
4 parts Chile flakes
2 parts Orange peel
2 parts Sesame seed
1 part Pepper, Sichuan
1 part Poppy seed
1 part Nori
1 part Ginger
