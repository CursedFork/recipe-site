Name: Madras Curry Powder
Aka: Madras curry, hot curry powder
Cuisines: Indian, British
Origin: India
Uses: Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Sauté or stir-fry, Simmer or boil, Braise or stew
Classic: Chicken, Curry, Beef
Also: Lamb, Lentils, Eggs
Tip: Fry it in oil before adding liquid. It is hotter than ordinary curry powder.
Typical version: One chef's curry powder, as the page describes it: black pepper, cinnamon, clove, coriander, cumin, fennel, fenugreek, mustard seed and turmeric. A hotter chile is added here for the Madras style.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madras_curry
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page gives one chef's spice list and an older recipe, and the exact parts are mine. A hotter chile is my addition for the Madras name.

Ingredients:
4 parts Coriander
3 parts Cumin
3 parts Turmeric
2 parts Chile, hot red
1 part Fenugreek
1 part Mustard seed
1 part Fennel seed
1 part Pepper, black
1/2 part Cinnamon
1/2 part Clove
