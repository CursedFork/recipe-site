Name: Celery Salt
Aka: celery seed salt
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Table seasoning
Methods: Raw or finishing
Classic: Tomatoes, Potatoes
Also: Salad dressings, Eggs, Cabbage and Brussels sprouts
Tip: Sprinkle it on tomatoes, potatoes and eggs, or into a bloody Mary. Cut back on regular salt when you use it.
Typical version: Celery seed and table salt in roughly equal parts, though shop versions are mostly salt.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celery_salt
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page covers celery salt as a seasoning, and I did not find a published ratio. The parts are mine.

Ingredients:
1 part Celery seed
2 parts Salt
