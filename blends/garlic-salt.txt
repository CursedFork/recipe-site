Name: Garlic Salt
Aka: garlic and salt
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Table seasoning, Dry rub
Methods: Raw or finishing, Roast, Grill
Classic: Bread and toast, Potatoes, Chicken
Also: Popcorn, Pasta, Steak
Tip: Great on garlic bread, fries and roasted potatoes. Use it in place of both the salt and the garlic.
Typical version: Dried, ground garlic and table salt, as the page says. Shop versions are mostly salt.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garlic_salt
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page describes garlic salt as a mixture of dried ground garlic and table salt. I did not find a published ratio, and the parts are mine.

Ingredients:
1 part Garlic powder
3 parts Salt
