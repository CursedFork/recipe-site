Name: Chaat Masala
Aka: chat masala, chaat powder
Cuisines: Indian, Pakistani
Origin: India
Uses: Finishing sprinkle, Table seasoning
Methods: Raw or finishing
Classic: Potatoes, Chickpeas, Fruit
Also: Yogurt, Cooked fruit, Snack mixes
Tip: Sprinkle it on at the very end, over cut fruit, boiled potatoes, chickpeas or yogurt. It is tangy and salty, so taste before adding salt.
Typical version: Amchur leads, with cumin and coriander behind it, and black salt for the eggy tang. The page also lists dried ginger, black pepper, asafoetida and chili powder.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaat_masala
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The ingredients come from the source, but the source gives no amounts. The parts are a typical home ratio that I chose, so treat them as a starting point to taste and adjust.

Ingredients:
4 parts Amchur
3 parts Cumin
2 parts Coriander
2 parts Black salt
1 part Pepper, black
1 part Ginger
1 part Chile, hot red
1/2 part Asafoetida (optional)
