Name: Tandoori Masala
Aka: tandoori spice, tandoori seasoning, tandoori powder
Cuisines: Indian, Pakistani
Origin: India
Uses: Wet marinade or paste, Dry rub
Methods: Grill, Roast
Classic: Chicken, Lamb, Cauliflower and broccoli
Also: Fish, Shrimp, Tofu
Tip: Stir it into yogurt with lemon juice, ginger and garlic, marinate the meat for several hours, then roast hot.
Typical version: The page says it typically has garam masala, garlic, ginger, onion and cayenne. Garam masala is itself a blend, so it is built here from its spices, plus Kashmiri chile for the red color.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tandoori_masala
Confidence: low
Doubt: The page gives only a loose list of components. The way I split garam masala into its spices, and every part amount, are my own, so this is the least certain of the new blends.

Ingredients:
3 parts Coriander
3 parts Cumin
3 parts Chile, mild red: Kashmiri
2 parts Ginger
2 parts Garlic powder
2 parts Onion powder
1 part Cinnamon
1 part Cardamom
1 part Pepper, black
1/2 part Clove
1 part Chile, hot red: Cayenne
