Name: Jerk Seasoning
Aka: jerk spice, Jamaican jerk, dry jerk rub
Cuisines: Jamaican, Caribbean
Origin: Jamaica
Uses: Dry rub, Wet marinade or paste
Methods: Grill, Smoke, Roast
Classic: Chicken, Pork, Pork ribs
Also: Fish, Sweet potatoes, Corn
Tip: Mix with oil, lime juice or soy sauce into a paste, rub it in, and rest it for a few hours. Grill slowly over a moderate fire.
Typical version: Allspice and thyme lead. Scotch bonnet is the traditional chile, and cayenne stands in here because fresh chile is not in this library.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerk_(cooking)
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page names allspice and Scotch bonnet peppers as the main ingredients of the jerk seasoning. The other spices and the parts are a typical dry version that I chose.

Ingredients:
3 parts Allspice
3 parts Thyme
2 parts Garlic powder
2 parts Onion powder
1 part Chile, hot red: Cayenne
1 part Ginger
1 part Pepper, black
1 part Sugar
1/2 part Cinnamon
1/2 part Nutmeg
1 part Salt (optional)
