Name: Pav Bhaji Masala
Aka: pav bhaji spice, bhaji masala
Cuisines: Indian
Origin: India
Uses: Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Sauté or stir-fry, Simmer or boil
Classic: Potatoes, Cauliflower and broccoli, Bread and toast
Also: Tomatoes, Bell peppers, sandwich
Tip: Add it to the onion, tomato and pepper base before the mashed vegetables go in. Add extra red chile powder in the pan for more heat.
Typical version: Coriander and cumin lead, with dried red chile, amchur, fennel, black pepper, cinnamon, clove, black and green cardamom and star anise.
Source: https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/homemade-pav-bhaji-masala-powder/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Indian Healthy Recipes), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is half a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. The recipe counts whole chiles, cloves and cardamom pods, which I turned into parts by estimate.

Ingredients:
18 parts Coriander
15 parts Cumin
8 parts Chile, hot red
6 parts Amchur
6 parts Fennel seed
4 parts Pepper, black
4 parts Cinnamon
4 parts Black cardamom
2 parts Clove
2 parts Star anise
2 parts Cardamom (optional)
1 part Bay leaf (optional)
1 part Ginger (optional)
