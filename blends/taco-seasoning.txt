Name: Taco Seasoning
Aka: taco spice, taco mix, homemade taco seasoning
Cuisines: Tex-Mex, Mexican, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub, Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Sauté or stir-fry, Simmer or boil, Grill
Classic: Ground beef, taco, Chicken
Also: Beans and lentils, Rice, Cauliflower and broccoli
Tip: Stir 1 to 2 tablespoons into a pound of cooked ground meat with a splash of water. It is also good on beans, soup and roasted vegetables.
Typical version: A chili powder base with cumin, smoked paprika, garlic and onion, plus oregano, black pepper, red pepper flakes and salt.
Source: https://www.loveandlemons.com/taco-seasoning-recipe/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Love and Lemons), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a quarter teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. Chili powder is itself a blend, so the mild red chile stands in for it here.

Ingredients:
12 parts Chile, mild red
4 parts Cumin
2 parts Garlic powder
2 parts Onion powder
2 parts Paprika: Smoked
1 part Oregano
1 part Pepper, black
1 part Chile flakes (optional)
1 part Salt
