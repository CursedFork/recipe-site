Name: Memphis Dry Rub
Aka: Memphis rub, Memphis-style dry rub, Memphis BBQ rub
Cuisines: American BBQ, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub
Methods: Smoke, Grill, Roast
Classic: Pork ribs, Pulled pork, Chicken
Also: Pork chops, Brisket and beef ribs
Tip: Rub it inside and out of a chicken or over 3 to 4 pounds of ribs. Memphis ribs are served dry, with no sauce, so the rub is the flavor.
Typical version: Mostly paprika with sugar, salt, celery, black pepper, mustard, onion, garlic and cayenne.
Source: https://www.kentuckyliving.com/recipes/memphis-style-dry-rub
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Kentucky Living), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. The recipe also uses celery salt and dry mustard, which I turned into celery seed, salt and ground mustard seed. The recipe uses brown sugar, which is not in this library, so plain sugar stands in.

Ingredients:
12 parts Paprika
8 parts Sugar
3 parts Salt
1 part Celery seed
1 part Pepper, black
1 part Mustard seed
1 part Onion powder
1 part Garlic powder
1 part Chile, hot red: Cayenne
