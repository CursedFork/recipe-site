Name: Mixed Spice
Aka: pudding spice, British mixed spice
Cuisines: British
Origin: United Kingdom
Uses: Sweet or baking
Methods: Bake
Classic: Cakes and muffins, Sweet breads and buns, Dried fruit
Also: Cookies, Pies and tarts, Cooked fruit
Tip: Add a teaspoon to fruit cakes, hot cross buns and puddings. It is the British cousin of pumpkin pie spice, with more coriander and allspice.
Typical version: Cinnamon and allspice lead, with nutmeg, ginger and clove. Coriander and mace are common extras.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_spice
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page describes it as a British blend of sweet spices, similar to pumpkin pie spice. The exact spices and parts are my typical version, since recipes vary.

Ingredients:
4 parts Cinnamon
2 parts Allspice
1 part Nutmeg
1 part Ginger
1/2 part Clove
1 part Coriander (optional)
1/2 part Mace (optional)
