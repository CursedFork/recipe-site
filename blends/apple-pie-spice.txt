Name: Apple Pie Spice
Aka: apple spice, apple pie seasoning
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Sweet or baking
Methods: Bake
Classic: Apples and pears, Pies and tarts, Cakes and muffins
Also: Oats and porridge, Coffee and hot chocolate, Mulled wine and cider
Tip: Use it one for one wherever a recipe calls for apple pie spice. Try it in crisps, muffins, oatmeal, pancakes and warm cider.
Typical version: Mostly cinnamon, with ginger, nutmeg and cardamom. Allspice and clove are common in other versions.
Source: https://www.loveandlemons.com/apple-pie-spice/
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Love and Lemons), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a quarter teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. Other recipes use allspice, which is left out here.

Ingredients:
24 parts Cinnamon
2 parts Ginger
1 part Nutmeg
1 part Cardamom
