Name: Cape Malay Curry Powder
Aka: Cape curry powder, Bo-Kaap curry powder, Cape Malay masala
Cuisines: South African
Origin: South Africa
Uses: Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Braise or stew, Simmer or boil
Classic: Chicken, Lamb, Curry
Also: Fish, Beans and lentils, Rice
Tip: Dry-roast the whole seeds for a minute or two, grind them, then mix in the ground spices. It is sweet and fragrant, not very hot.
Typical version: Coriander leads, with cumin, fenugreek, cardamom, turmeric, black pepper, fennel, mustard, clove, ginger, dried red chile and curry leaf.
Source: https://www.food.com/recipe/bo-kaap-cape-malay-curry-powder-south-african-spice-mixture-289383
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Food.com), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ. The recipe counts curry leaves and whole chiles, which I turned into parts by estimate. It is a user recipe, not a published cookbook one.

Ingredients:
24 parts Coriander
12 parts Cardamom
12 parts Turmeric
9 parts Cumin
9 parts Fenugreek
6 parts Pepper, black
3 parts Fennel seed
3 parts Mustard seed
3 parts Clove
3 parts Ginger
3 parts Chile, hot red
1 part Curry leaf
