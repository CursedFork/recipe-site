Name: Poultry Seasoning
Aka: poultry spice, stuffing seasoning, poultry herbs
Cuisines: American, British
Origin: United States
Uses: Dry rub, Table seasoning
Methods: Roast, Bake, Sauté or stir-fry
Classic: Turkey, Chicken
Also: Pork chops, Potatoes, Gravy and pan sauces
Tip: Rub it on poultry before roasting, or stir it into stuffing, gravy and soups. It is the classic Thanksgiving flavor.
Typical version: Sage and thyme lead, with marjoram and rosemary, a little nutmeg and white pepper.
Source: https://www.jocooks.com/wprm_print/poultry-seasoning
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The proportions come from one recipe page that I read (Jo Cooks), with the amounts converted to parts (1 part is a quarter teaspoon). Seasoning blends have no single standard, and other recipes differ.

Ingredients:
8 parts Sage
8 parts Thyme
4 parts Marjoram
4 parts Rosemary
2 parts Pepper, white
1 part Nutmeg
