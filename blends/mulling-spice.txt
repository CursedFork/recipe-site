Name: Mulling Spice
Aka: mulling spices, mulled wine spice, mulled cider spice
Cuisines: British, German, American
Origin: Germany
Uses: Drink or infusion
Methods: Simmer or boil
Classic: Mulled wine and cider
Also: Tea and chai, Cooked fruit
Tip: Use whole spices if you can and simmer gently, never at a hard boil, then strain. Ground spices turn a drink cloudy.
Typical version: Cinnamon, clove, allspice and nutmeg, and sometimes star anise, peppercorn, cardamom and orange peel.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulling_spices
Confidence: medium
Doubt: Mulled wine is a northern European tradition, so Germany is a broad pointer. The page says it usually consists of cinnamon, cloves, allspice and nutmeg, and less often star anise, peppercorn or cardamom. The parts are mine.

Ingredients:
4 parts Cinnamon
2 parts Allspice
2 parts Orange peel (optional)
1 part Clove
1 part Nutmeg
1 part Star anise (optional)
