Name: Everything Bagel Seasoning
Aka: everything seasoning, everything topping, everything bagel topping
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Table seasoning, Finishing sprinkle
Methods: Raw or finishing, Bake
Classic: Bread and toast, Scrambled and fried eggs
Also: Dips and spreads, Popcorn, Chips and crackers
Tip: Shake it on avocado toast, eggs, cream cheese, popcorn or roasted vegetables. Sesame is a common allergen.
Typical version: Sesame, poppy seed, onion, garlic and salt, which are the usual bagel toppings. Dried minced onion and garlic are the traditional forms, and powders stand in here.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everything_bagel
Confidence: low
Doubt: The page describes the bagel and its toppings, and not a shaker mix. The parts and the shaker form are my own, and it is a loose reading of that source.

Ingredients:
3 parts Sesame seed
3 parts Poppy seed
2 parts Onion powder
2 parts Garlic powder
2 parts Salt
