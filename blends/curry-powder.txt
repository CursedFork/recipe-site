Name: Curry Powder
Aka: British curry powder, yellow curry powder, curry masala
Cuisines: British, Indian
Origin: United Kingdom
Uses: Cooking base or sauce, Dry rub
Methods: Sauté or stir-fry, Braise or stew, Simmer or boil
Classic: Chicken, Curry, Potatoes
Also: Lentils, Eggs, Rice
Tip: Fry it in oil for a minute before adding liquid, because raw curry powder tastes dusty. Turmeric makes it yellow.
Typical version: A mild everyday version built from coriander, turmeric and cumin, with fenugreek, ginger, black pepper and chile for depth and heat. There is no single standard, and the commercial products differ a lot.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curry_powder
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page confirms that coriander, chilli, mustard seed, pepper and cumin appear in curry powders. The turmeric-heavy proportions here are my typical home ratio and not a published standard.

Ingredients:
4 parts Coriander
3 parts Turmeric
3 parts Cumin
1 part Fenugreek
1 part Ginger
1 part Pepper, black
1 part Chile, hot red
