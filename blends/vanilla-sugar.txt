Name: Vanilla Sugar
Aka: vanilla-scented sugar
Cuisines: German, French, Scandinavian
Origin: Germany
Uses: Sweet or baking
Methods: Bake
Classic: Cakes and muffins, Cookies, Custards and puddings
Also: Berries, Coffee and hot chocolate, Pies and tarts
Tip: Bury a split vanilla bean in a jar of sugar and wait two weeks. Use it in place of plain sugar in bakes and drinks.
Typical version: The page describes one to two cups of white sugar aged with vanilla pods for two weeks.
