Name: Pumpkin Pie Spice
Aka: pumpkin spice, pumpkin pie seasoning
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Sweet or baking
Methods: Bake
Classic: Pies and tarts, Cakes and muffins, Custards and puddings
Also: Oats and porridge, Coffee and hot chocolate, Cookies
Tip: Use a teaspoon or so in a pie, quick bread or oatmeal. It is already a mix, so there is no need to add the other spices.
Typical version: Mostly cinnamon, with ginger, nutmeg and clove, and allspice if you like it.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumpkin_pie_spice
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says it is generally a blend of cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, cloves, and sometimes allspice. The parts are a common home ratio that I chose.

Ingredients:
4 parts Cinnamon
2 parts Ginger
1 part Nutmeg
1 part Clove
1 part Allspice (optional)
