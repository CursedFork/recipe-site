Name: Onion Salt
Aka: onion and salt
Cuisines: American
Origin: United States
Uses: Table seasoning, Dry rub
Methods: Raw or finishing, Roast, Grill
Classic: Chips and crackers, Potatoes, Ground beef
Also: Dips and spreads, Popcorn, Chicken
Tip: Use it for burgers, dips and fries, in place of the salt and the onion. It clumps in humid kitchens, so keep it sealed.
Typical version: Dried onion and salt, as the page says is the primary two ingredients.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion_salt
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page describes onion salt as dried onion and salt, with no ratio. The parts are mine, and onion powder stands in for dried onion.

Ingredients:
1 part Onion powder
