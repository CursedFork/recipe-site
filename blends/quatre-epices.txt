Name: Quatre Epices
Aka: quatre épices, four spices, French four-spice
Cuisines: French
Origin: France
Uses: Dry rub, Table seasoning
Methods: Roast, Braise or stew
Classic: Pork, Sausage, Stews and braises
Also: Ham, Duck, Soup
Tip: Use it in pates, sausages, stews and cured meats. It is white-pepper-heavy and clove-strong, so measure small pinches.
Typical version: Mostly white pepper, with clove, nutmeg and ginger. The page notes that some recipes use roughly equal parts, and others use more pepper.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quatre_%C3%A9pices
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page says most blends use a larger share of white pepper, usually, and some cooks use cinnamon or allspice. The exact parts here are my typical ratio.

Ingredients:
8 parts Pepper, white
1 part Clove
1 part Nutmeg
1 part Ginger
