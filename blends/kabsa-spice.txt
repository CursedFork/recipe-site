Name: Kabsa Spice
Aka: kabsa spice mix, Saudi rice spice, machboos spice
Cuisines: Gulf Arab
Origin: Saudi Arabia
Uses: Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Simmer or boil, Braise or stew
Classic: Rice, Chicken, Pilaf and biryani
Also: Lamb, Goat meat
Tip: Stir it into the rice and chicken with the cooking liquid. Dried lime is added whole or ground and gives the sour note.
Typical version: Cardamom, cinnamon, dried lime, bay leaf and nutmeg, with a little saffron. The page names cardamom, saffron, cinnamon, black lime, bay leaves and nutmeg.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabsa
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The ingredients come from the source, but the source gives no amounts. The parts are a typical home ratio that I chose, so treat them as a starting point to taste and adjust.

Ingredients:
2 parts Cardamom
2 parts Cinnamon
2 parts Dried lime
1 part Bay leaf
1/2 part Nutmeg
1/2 part Saffron (optional)
