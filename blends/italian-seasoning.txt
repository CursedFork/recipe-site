Name: Italian Seasoning
Aka: Italian herbs, Italian herb mix
Cuisines: Italian, American
Origin: United States
Uses: Table seasoning, Dry rub
Methods: Bake, Roast, Simmer or boil
Classic: Tomato sauces, Pizza, Pasta
Also: Chicken, Bread and toast, Soup
Tip: Stir it into tomato sauce early, or sprinkle it on pizza and bread before baking. Dried herbs need a little time in the heat.
Typical version: The five herbs the page names as the base: basil, oregano, rosemary, thyme and marjoram.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_seasoning
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page confirms the herbs. The parts are my typical ratio, and the mix as a pre-blended product is an American idea more than an Italian one.

Ingredients:
3 parts Oregano
3 parts Basil
2 parts Thyme
2 parts Rosemary
1 part Marjoram
