Name: Paella Seasoning
Aka: paella spice, paella spice mix
Cuisines: Spanish
Origin: Spain
Uses: Cooking base or sauce
Methods: Simmer or boil, Bake
Classic: Rice, Shellfish, Chicken
Also: Pilaf and biryani, Shrimp, Game birds
Tip: Stir it into the rice with the stock. Saffron gives the traditional color, and paprika does most of the work if you use little.
Typical version: Sweet paprika, saffron, rosemary, garlic and salt, as the page lists for the traditional paella.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paella
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page lists the paella ingredients, and I turned the dry ones into a mix. The parts are mine.

Ingredients:
4 parts Paprika
1 part Rosemary
1 part Garlic powder
1/2 part Saffron
1 part Salt (optional)
