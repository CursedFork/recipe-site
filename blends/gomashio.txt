Name: Gomashio
Aka: sesame salt, goma shio
Cuisines: Japanese
Origin: Japan
Uses: Table seasoning, Finishing sprinkle
Methods: Raw or finishing
Classic: Rice
Also: Vegetables, Noodles, Tofu
Tip: Toast the sesame seeds first, then crush them lightly with the salt so the oils come out. Use it as a table salt.
Typical version: Toasted sesame with salt, often about ten parts sesame to one part salt.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gomashio
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page confirms sesame and salt. The ratio of about ten to one is a common one that I could not confirm on a page.

Ingredients:
10 parts Sesame seed
1 part Salt
