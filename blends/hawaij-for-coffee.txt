Name: Hawaij for Coffee
Aka: hawayej for coffee, Yemeni coffee spice, qishr spice
Cuisines: Yemeni
Origin: Yemen
Uses: Drink or infusion
Methods: Simmer or boil
Classic: Coffee and hot chocolate
Also: Tea and chai
Tip: Simmer a spoonful with coffee grounds or in a coffee pot, and strain. It tastes warmer and sweeter than plain coffee.
Typical version: The page says the mixture for coffee is made from aniseeds, fennel seeds, ginger and cardamom. Aniseed is not in this library, so fennel and ginger and cardamom carry it here.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawaij
Confidence: medium
Doubt: The page lists aniseed, fennel, ginger and cardamom. I left aniseed out, and the parts are mine.

Ingredients:
2 parts Fennel seed
2 parts Ginger
2 parts Cardamom
