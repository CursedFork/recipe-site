# Blend roster (Phase 4, step 1)

**Status: for you to strike from. No blend files have been written yet.**

This is the list of blends I believe are real and documented enough to write up with a source. I will not write a blend I cannot source, so the count is whatever survives, not a target. Strike anything you do not want, add anything I missed, then I start the first 25.

## How I chose

- **A dry blend you could make from jars.** Pastes and marinades (Thai curry paste, harissa paste, chermoula, jerk marinade, gochujang) are left out because they are not made from a spice cabinet alone. Say so if you want them in.
- **Brand names left out.** Old Bay, Tajin, Goya Sazon and Kitchen King are products, so I list the generic idea behind each ("Crab boil seasoning", "Chile-lime seasoning") and mark it *generic*.
- **Documented, not invented.** Each name below has a recipe or description in more than one place that I know of. I have not yet read the sources for each one. Reading them is the work of writing the batch, and any that fail that check get dropped and reported.
- **The honest number.** There are **145** names below. I do not expect all of them to survive sourcing, and I would rather stop near 140 than pad to 200 with look-alikes (for example "Cajun seasoning 2"). Your spec said to stop at the honest number, so the roster will end there unless you add names.

## Notes on the list

- **Some names overlap on purpose.** "Garam masala" and "Bengali garam masala" are different recipes, but "Taco seasoning", "Burrito seasoning" and "Taco bowl seasoning" are close, and the sources may show they are the same thing. I will merge any that turn out to be duplicates, and tell you.
- **Cinnamon sugar and Vanilla sugar** are simple two-ingredient blends. They are real, but you may want them out because they are barely spice blends.
- **Weakest names** (most likely to be dropped for lack of a source): Kerala meat masala, Vietnamese five-spice, Gulf spice (bzar), Armenian lavash spice, Peruvian aji seasoning, Hot chocolate spice, Chai-style baking spice, Pumpkin spice latte spice, Roasted vegetable seasoning, Popcorn seasoning, Taco bowl seasoning, Burger seasoning, Fish seasoning, Hot wing seasoning.
